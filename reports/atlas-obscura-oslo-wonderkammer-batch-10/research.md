# Atlas Obscura Oslo Wonderkammer batch 10

## Representation

Three Atlas Obscura discoveries are modeled as `actual_site_treasure` Wonderkammer chambers attached to existing canonical places:

- Grass Roots Square → `regjeringskvartalet`
- Santa → `ekebergparken`
- Untuned Bell → `radhusplassen`

## Grass Roots Square

Atlas Obscura's historic Teatergata location is outdated. KORO documents that Do Ho Suh's installation was dismantled, restored and relocated to Einar Gerhardsens plass in the new Government Quarter. The new Government Quarter opened in April 2026, and KORO's current artwork record places the work there.

The History Go entry therefore records the 2026 placement and explicitly warns against reusing the old R6/Teatergata location.

## Santa

Ekebergparken's official artwork page identifies Paul McCarthy's `Santa` as a 2018 painted-bronze work measuring 620 x 300 x 325 cm. The park interprets the ambiguous object held by the figure through McCarthy's broader practice of distorting familiar popular-cultural icons and criticizing consumer culture.

## Untuned Bell

KORO documents A K Dolven's interactive work at Honnørbrygga. The bell was removed from Oslo City Hall in 2000 for being out of tune with the other 48 bells and returned to the area as an artwork in 2020. A ground pedal lets visitors give the bell a voice.

## Data rule

No new canonical map markers are introduced. All three objects are discoverable details within existing parent places.
