## Policy basis

The completed VisitOSLO Oslo West closure established that private commercial galleries, shops, showrooms and other high-churn visitor listings are not mandatory canonical places in the current bounded completeness model. The policy avoids opportunistic one-off inclusion and reserves these venues for a dedicated commercial-gallery / art-venue framework based on longevity, independent place identity, cultural significance and coordinate stability.

Fineart Oslo was explicitly deferred by the prior Oslo West and museum-source closures. The same policy is applied consistently here to Galleri Brandstrup, Galleri Haaken, Peder Lund, Pushwagner Gallery and M35.

This is a classification decision, not a judgment that these venues can never become History Go places. They may be reconsidered together under a dedicated, consistent gallery-inclusion policy.
