# Fotografiens Hus — taxonomy decision

Date: 2026-07-20

## Decision

- Canonical id: `fotografiens_hus`
- Primary category: `kunst`
- Secondary analytical layers: photography as medium and profession, curation, visual culture, public exhibition practice
- Physical model: one stable gallery/institution place at Rådhusgata 20

## Why `kunst`

Fotografiens Hus describes itself as a public photography gallery and has operated as a dedicated exhibition venue in Rådhusgata 20 since 1999. Its stated purpose is to increase knowledge of and engagement with photography as expression and professional field, and its recurring programme presents photography through curated exhibitions. The durable place identity is therefore an art institution centred on a specific medium, not a generic event venue, commercial photo shop, museum collection or city-area marker.

## Canonical scope

The place record should represent the institution and its stable public exhibition function. Individual temporary exhibitions must remain content layers rather than separate overlapping places.

Recommended canonical emne anchors:

- `em_kunst_institusjonskritikk_og_representasjon`
- `em_kunst_kvalitet_kritikk_og_symbolsk_kapital`

These should organise later quiz/theory work, while visible factual quiz content must continue to be driven by external sources under the source-priority rules.

## Content guardrails

The record should foreground:

- Fotografiens Hus as a dedicated public photography gallery
- continuous use of Rådhusgata 20 as its exhibition venue since 1999
- photography as both artistic expression and professional practice
- curation and the recurring exhibition programme
- the institution's role as a meeting and knowledge-sharing place for photographers and audiences

Avoid:

- reducing the place to whichever temporary exhibition is currently on view
- generic statements about photography without a place or institutional anchor
- treating the building itself as a separate canonical place without a distinct source-driven case
- presenting the gallery as a museum with a permanent historical collection

## Production gate

Coordinate and physical-overlap gates are already resolved in the merged intake. Taxonomy is now locked and `fotografiens_hus` is ready for canonical production as one `kunst` place.
