# Kampen Økologiske Barnebondegård — canonical taxonomy decision

Date: 2026-07-20

## Decision

**Primary category: `by`**  
**Secondary category: `natur`**  
**Hybrid: `true`**

Model the whole site as one canonical physical place, `kampen_okologiske_barnebondegard`, at the verified Skedsmogata 23 complex.

## Why `by` is primary

- The farm was created as a neighbourhood project initiated by children and opened in 1994 on a former school-garden site. Its defining identity is therefore a deliberately created urban social institution rather than a preserved historic farmstead.
- The current official purpose is explicitly child-focused and community-oriented: a low-threshold place to be, learn, meet others, experience belonging and take part in meaningful tasks across age, culture and functional ability.
- The municipality describes a high-volume public and pedagogical visitor function with organised school, kindergarten and group visits. The site also supports alternative school days, work training, youth work and adapted activities.
- This makes the farm best understood in History Go as urban social and learning infrastructure: a concrete example of how neighbourhood space can be organised for inclusion, participation, care, food knowledge and everyday community life.

## Why `natur` is secondary, not primary

- Animals, plants, cultivation, food production and natural cycles are central learning content and justify a real secondary nature layer.
- The site is nevertheless a managed urban farm. Domestic animals must not be treated as evidence of a wild habitat, and the farm must not be represented as a generic nature area.
- Nature questions should be anchored in documented cultivation, soil, plant growth, food production, animal care and ecological cycles at the farm. They must not invent local wild-species observations or imply that managed livestock represent natural fauna.

## Why not `historie`, `sport` or `vitenskap`

- `historie` is not primary because the physical object is not principally a preserved historic farm complex such as Geitmyra or Vøienvolden. The 1991–1994 neighbourhood-project history is important content, but not a heritage-object identity.
- `sport` is not primary because riding is one adapted activity among many; unlike EKT, organised equestrian activity is not the continuous core institutional function.
- `vitenskap` is not primary because the site is a pedagogical and social municipal/community offer rather than a research, collection or science institution.

## Recommended canonical emne path

Primary by-emner:

- `em_by_inkludering_ekskludering`

The place profile should also preserve play, informal activity and social mixing as question angles, but visible quiz claims must be driven by external official/local sources rather than generated directly from emne labels.

## Representation rules

1. Create one map marker for the integrated Skedsmogata 23 farm site.
2. Do not split the farmyard, school garden, barn, stable, open-farm programme or child/youth programmes into overlapping places.
3. Do not merge the site with `kampen_kirke` or Kampen Park activity places; they are physically separate.
4. Do not classify domestic animals as wild nature observations.
5. Keep the origin story — neighbourhood conference in 1991 and opening in 1994 — as a major place-specific history layer.
6. Treat current opening as seasonally variable; coordinate verification does not imply that the farm is open every day.
