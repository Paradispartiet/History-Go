# Politikk / Politisk rådgivning — prerequisite source-first closure

## Scope

- This package closes the runtime prerequisite gaps around day one, People, Places, mail, knowledge and workday-loop evidence for `politikk_politisk_radgivning`.
- It preserves `Politisk rådgiver` as `appointment_required` with qualification `employer_appointment`; it never converts the role to direct badge unlock.
- It is prerequisite completion, not Role World completion. The dedicated Role World remains a separate one-role rollout PR.

## Canonical evidence and authority

- `data/Civication/politikkCareerLifeEvidence.json` already classifies Politisk rådgiver as a formal political staff position behind employer appointment.
- The adviser may analyze, recommend, prepare meetings and communication, and follow up only within the actual employment role and access boundaries.
- Advice, proximity to political leadership, popularity, History Go knowledge or situated reputation is never appointment, delegation, evidence or a public decision.

## Materialized foundation

- Four fictional scenario actors and four political-advisory work surfaces.
- Persistent work object: `radgivningslogg_bestilling_fakta_alternativer_risiko_rad_beslutning_og_oppfolging` with explicit waiting, handoff and bounded rework.
- Fifteen source mails across all nine canonical mail types: 4 job, 4 people and one each of conflict, story, event, micro, followup, knowledge and consequence.
- Sixteen-step mail plan with no generic fallback.
- History Go can improve historical and institutional questions about political decision-making, but cannot replace current sources, legal or budget evidence, confidentiality controls, employer_appointment, delegation or the actual decision owner.
