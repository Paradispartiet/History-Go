# Politikk / Organisasjonsarbeid — prerequisite source-first closure

## Scope

- This package closes the nine runtime prerequisite gaps around day one, People, Places, mail, knowledge, workday loop and the corresponding realism foundations.
- It preserves the canonical `direct` career offer for Organisasjonssekretær; it does not invent an appointment or qualification gate.
- It is prerequisite completion, not Role World completion. The dedicated Role World remains a separate one-role rollout PR.

## Canonical authority boundary

- Secretariat work may prepare meetings, coordinate activity, answer members, document decisions and follow actual decisions within delegated administrative scope.
- Secretariat work may not rewrite decisions, use member data without a legitimate work basis, bind money or contracts without authority, or speak as an elected body without mandate.
- Politics Badge, XP, History Go knowledge and situated reputation remain learning/standing signals, never formal authority.

## Materialized foundation

- Four fictional scenario actors and four role-owned workplace surfaces.
- Persistent work object: `organisasjonslogg_medlem_mote_vedtak_aktivitet_og_oppfolging` with explicit waiting, handoff and bounded rework.
- Fifteen source mails across all nine canonical mail types: 4 job, 4 people and one each of conflict, story, event, micro, followup, knowledge and consequence.
- Sixteen-step mail plan with no generic fallback.
- History Go affords better institutional and historical questions but cannot create mandate, consent, data access, budget authority, contract authority or an organizational decision.
