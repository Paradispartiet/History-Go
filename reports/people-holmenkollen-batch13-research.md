## Scope

Five additional canonical people records for `holmenkollen_nasjonalanlegg`, selected only when there is a direct documented individual Holmenkollen competition victory.

## Canonical audit

Fresh repository searches against current `main` were run before creation for IDs, full names and relevant spelling variants:
- `vladimir_podzimek` / Vladimir Podzimek / Podzimek
- `toni_nieminen` / Toni Nieminen
- `andreas_goldberger` / Andreas Goldberger
- `kazuyoshi_funaki` / Kazuyoshi Funaki
- `primoz_peterka` / Primož Peterka / Primoz Peterka

No existing canonical people records were found for the five selected profiles.

Erik Johnsen was considered but not selected because the name produced existing repository hits during the canonical audit and was therefore excluded from this batch rather than risking a duplicate or identity collision.

## Place gate

Every record is anchored by a direct individual ski jumping victory in Holmenkollen. General ski-jumping fame, championship results elsewhere, medal awards alone, or broad Oslo associations are not sufficient for inclusion.
