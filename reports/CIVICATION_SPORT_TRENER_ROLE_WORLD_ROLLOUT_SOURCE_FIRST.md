## Situated reputation contract

There is no global reputation score. Standing must be audience-specific and may diverge across at least these audiences:

- `sports_leadership`
- `coaching_staff`
- `players`
- `performance_analysis`
- `player_development_and_load_support`
- `club_leadership`
- `public_external_pressure`
- `private_relations`

Legitimate divergence includes:

- Holding documented selection criteria can strengthen coaching-staff and sports-leadership trust while a disappointed player or external audience remains dissatisfied.
- Explaining a reduced role honestly can strengthen player trust even when short-term status around the player falls.
- Respecting an externally defined load boundary can strengthen support-staff trust while making the match plan harder and reducing perceived decisiveness from result-focused observers.
- Refusing a panic reset after losses can strengthen analysis trust while public pressure interprets the same restraint as passivity.
- Admitting that a training design needs revision can cost short-term head-coach status while strengthening longer-term player-development credibility.

Standing can never grant or expand formal authority. It cannot:

- authorize diagnosis, treatment or medical clearance;
- override a load or health boundary owned by another qualified role;
- create contract, employment or commercial authority;
- expand selection authority beyond the trainer's actual delegated mandate;
- turn public popularity, player affection or club status into a sports criterion;
- transfer the athlete's role scope or evidence ownership to the trainer;
- let the trainer overwrite the athlete's observations merely because of hierarchy;
- turn Badge progression or `Hovedtrener` standing into a legal, medical or organizational mandate.

## Cross-role boundary

`sport/sport_utover` is a genuine candidate companion because both roles encounter the same training, load, selection and competition surfaces through different lenses. This PR keeps that relationship editorial-only:

- status: `candidate_when_shared_work_is_real`

The trainer may plan, observe, give feedback and make sports decisions only within actual mandate. The athlete retains their own body reports, experience and canonical role scope. A future shared object would require its own explicit governed proof; this rollout does not create it implicitly.
