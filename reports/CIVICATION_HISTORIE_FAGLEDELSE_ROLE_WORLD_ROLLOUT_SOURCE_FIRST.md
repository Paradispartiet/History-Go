# Civication Historie — Fagledelse Role World rollout

## Editorial uniqueness
This Role World is specifically about the social and epistemic burden of leading historical work: protecting evidence and method from conclusion pressure, making capacity and delegation visible, preserving professional dissent, owning correction and rework, and separating managerial standing from historical truth. It is not a generic management rewrite and it does not turn seniority, popularity or institutional status into evidence.

## Continuity
- Existing 16-step plan is preserved exactly.
- Existing `role_model_v2`, four fictional scenario actors, four work surfaces, work grammar, `faglig_prioriterings_og_kvalitetslogg`, waiting/handoff/rework rhythm and authority boundaries remain canonical.
- All 15 prerequisite mails across all nine canonical mail types are provenance for the 56-beat season.
- `appointment_required` remains authoritative: History Go progression cannot appoint a leader or grant line, personnel or budget authority.
- No new runtime state and no parallel scene format.

## Situated standing
There is no global reputation score. The same leadership decision can create different standing with senior historians and method peers, staff carrying capacity, the department/resource line, quality/archive/documentation interfaces, commissioners and institutional clients, public communication and affected groups, wider professional peers, and private relations. Standing never grants evidentiary truth, the right to dictate findings, permission to suppress dissent, line or budget authority, publication authority, source clearance, legal dispensation or personnel power.

## Season structure
- 14 days × four phases = 56 authored beats.
- Morning = task, lunch = relationship, afternoon = decision, evening = private consequence.
- 7 primary threads, 8 delayed consequences and 5 private aftermath arcs.
- Slow standing axes remain `editorial_only_until_governed`.
- Every one of the 15 canonical source mails is reused at least three times across the season.

## History Go
The existing `historie` knowledge affordance is bounded to source criticism, historiography, context discrimination and better questions. Historical context may reveal missing perspectives, periodization choices or source asymmetries; it cannot decide a present historical conclusion, certify a source, grant appointment, delegation, budget, publication or personnel authority, or make a History Go badge equivalent to professional competence.

## Cross-role
Readiness remains `candidate_when_shared_work_is_real`. No real shared work object with another role is proven in the prerequisite foundation, so no cross-role link or runtime is materialized merely because plausible handoffs exist.
