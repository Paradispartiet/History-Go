## Chronology decision

- the start of all mill activity;

2. early-modern water-powered industrial expansion;
3. surviving brick mill and factory buildings mainly from 1875 to 1920;
4. industrial relocation, demolition and the 1971 fire;
5. rehabilitation and adaptive reuse after 1998;
6. the museum's move into Kloster and Galla mills in 2000.
