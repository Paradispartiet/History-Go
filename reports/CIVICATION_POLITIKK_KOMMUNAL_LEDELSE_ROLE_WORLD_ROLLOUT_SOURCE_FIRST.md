# Politikk / Kommunal ledelse — Role World rollout source-first

## Scope lock
This is a one-role controlled rollout for `politikk/politikk_kommunal_ledelse`. Ordfører remains `appointment_required` and requires `election_or_mandate`; badge threshold, XP, popularity and standing never create office or authority.

## Authored delta
Only `situated_reputation` is newly authored. There is **no global reputation score**. Existing role model, People/Places, 16-step plan, persistent work object, work loops, waiting/handoff/rework and authority boundary are preserved. No new runtime and no parallel scene format are introduced.

## Audience model
Standing is separate for the collegial council, minority/negotiation counterparts, municipal administration, legal/habilitet/control functions, residents/local public, implementation/services, and private/party boundaries. Divergence is intentional: support, trust and criticism may move in different directions without becoming authority.

## Authority separation
History Go and Politikk-badge may improve questions about local political history, institutions and places, but cannot supply election_or_mandate, saksutredning, habilitetsavgjørelse, hjemmel, delegation or a municipal decision. Situated reputation cannot override a collegial vote, administrative integrity, or the municipality/party boundary.

## Cross-role
Cross-role need remains `not_required_for_rollout`; there is **no cross-role link** because no genuinely shared persistent work object is required for this role's rollout.

## Provenance and materialization
The season uses all **15 canonical** prerequisite mail references as provenance. The dramaturgical contract is **14 days × 4 phases = 56** unique beats, with every canonical ref used at least three times.

## Editorial uniqueness
The municipal world is authored around council procedure, minority rights, habilitet, administrative integrity, resident accountability, implementation and municipality/party boundaries. Structure may reuse the proven Role World contract, but text, social actors, standing audiences, threads and consequences are role-specific and do not copy the Natur / Politisk myndighet content.
