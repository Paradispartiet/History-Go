## Identity decision

## 1814 framing

On 8 August 1814, Christian Frederik held a council meeting at Spydeberg prestegård while the war with Sweden was under way. The discussions and decisions formed part of the sequence leading toward negotiations and the Convention of Moss on 14 August.

The record explicitly separates the three national sites and events:

- **Eidsvoll:** adoption of the Constitution;
- **Spydeberg:** council, headquarters and decision-making stage on 8 August;
- **Moss:** negotiations and Convention of Moss.

The place must not claim that the Constitution was adopted or that the Convention was signed at Spydeberg.

## Wilse and garden layer

Jacob Nicolai Wilse lived at the rectory from 1768 and developed a documented formal garden. Riksantikvaren and NIKU describe how the garden was reconstructed using Wilse's drawings and written descriptions, surviving landscape structures and garden-archaeological investigations.

This provides a distinct Enlightenment, science and garden-history layer alongside the 1814 political history.
