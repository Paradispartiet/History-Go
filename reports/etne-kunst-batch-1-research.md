# Etne kunst/kultur batch 1 — research

## Scope and canonical category

Working label: Etne kultur batch 1.

Canonical place category: `kunst`.

The repository uses `kunst` for culture houses, performance venues and similar public arts/culture places. No new `kultur` category is introduced.

## Duplicate audit

Current `main` was searched for IDs, names and relevant variants for:

- `skakke_kultursenter_etne`
- `skanevik_kultur_og_idrettshall`
- `house_of_blues_skanevik`
- `skanevik_fjordhotel_pippifestivalen`
- `musikkpaviljongen_doktorhagen`

No existing canonical place records were found for the five selected candidates.

## 1. Skakke – senter for kultur, skule og idrett

Sources:

- Etne kommune, organisation listing: Skakke – senter for kultur, skule og idrett, Strondavegen 30, 5590 Etne.
- Etne kulturskule: teaching at Skakkesenteret; music, theatre and art; concerts, performances and exhibitions.

Editorial decision:

Create one overarching place for the building and its culture functions. Do not create separate place markers for Etne kulturskule or the cinema in the same building.

Coordinate policy:

Resolve the official Kartverket/Geonorge address point for Strondavegen 30 during integration.

## 2. Skånevik kultur- og idrettshall

Sources:

- Etne kommune organisation listing: Ligrendvegen 11, 5593 Skånevik.
- Local coverage describes the hall as more than an idrettshall: a meeting place for culture, voluntary work, courses and major local events, built and sustained through strong dugnad engagement.

Editorial decision:

Classify under `kunst` for its documented culture and community-arena role; do not treat it only as a sports venue.

Coordinate policy:

Resolve the official Kartverket/Geonorge address point for Ligrendvegen 11 during integration.

## 3. House of Blues Skånevik

Sources:

- Skånevik Fjordhotel local guide describes House of Blues as the only permanent live stage for music and other cultural experiences in Skånevik, with concerts through the year.
- Skånevik Bluesfestival uses House of Blues as its permanent organisational/venue anchor.
- Venue listing gives Verven 20, 5593 Skånevik.

Editorial decision:

Create the permanent live-stage place, not a point claiming to represent the entire multi-venue Skånevik Bluesfestival.

Year note:

`year: 2007` refers to the establishment year of House of Blues AS, not the start of Skånevik Bluesfestival.

Coordinate policy:

Resolve the official Kartverket/Geonorge address point for Verven 20 during integration.

## 4. Skånevik Fjordhotel / Pippifestivalen

Sources:

- Skånevik Fjordhotel gives its address as Strondavegen 2695, 5593 Skånevik.
- The hotel and Pippifestivalen sources document the hotel garden as the recurring venue for Pippifestivalen, with theatre performances and family activities.
- The hotel also documents internal stage/event facilities.

Editorial decision:

The record is limited to the documented cultural/festival function. It is not a generic hotel place.

Coordinate policy:

Resolve the official Kartverket/Geonorge address point for Strondavegen 2695 during integration.

## 5. Musikkpaviljongen i Doktorhagen

Source:

- Skånevik Fjordhotel local guide documents that the pavilion was funded by local residents and Lions Club through lotteries, collections and events, inaugurated and transferred to Etne municipality on 3 June 2000, with an open invitation to local musicians to use it.

Editorial decision:

Strong independent culture place because it is a physical public music pavilion with a documented cultural purpose and history.

Coordinate policy:

No reliable machine-readable street address was found in the source set. The source coordinate is therefore an explicitly representative Doktorhagen-area anchor with a moderate radius. Integration must preserve this uncertainty disclosure and spatially validate it against the Skånevik centre/hotel area rather than pretending centimetre precision.

## Excluded/deferred

- Etne kulturskule as a separate place: excluded because its main Etne teaching location is inside Skakke.
- Etne kino as a separate place: excluded because it is a function within Skakke.
- Skånevik Bluesfestival as one generic map point: excluded because the festival uses multiple places; House of Blues is the concrete permanent venue anchor.
- Skånevik Fjordhotel as generic hospitality: not used; only its documented Pippifestival/theatre role is represented.
