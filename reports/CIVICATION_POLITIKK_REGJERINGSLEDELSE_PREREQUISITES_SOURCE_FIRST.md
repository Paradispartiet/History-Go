# Politikk / Regjeringsledelse — prerequisite source-first closure

## Scope

- This package closes day one, People, Places, mail, knowledge and workday-loop prerequisites for `politikk_regjeringsledelse`.
- It preserves three canonical badge titles in one role scope: `Statssekretær`, `Statsråd (minister)` and `Statsminister`.
- All three remain `appointment_required` with qualification `public_office_appointment`. A shared role scope never makes their authority interchangeable.
- This is prerequisite completion, not Role World completion.

## Authority and source boundary

- Existing Politics career regression proves that election_or_mandate alone and employer_appointment alone cannot materialize Statsminister; public_office_appointment is the hard gate for all three government-leadership titles.
- Statssekretær is bounded by actual assignment/delegation and cannot inherit the minister or prime minister role.
- Statsråd carries the concrete ministerial political leadership/sector responsibility only within law, formal decision processes and the appointed portfolio.
- Statsminister leads and coordinates government but is not modeled as an unlimited personal authority replacing ministers, collegial government, Parliament or other constitutional organs.
- History Go can improve historical/institutional questions about governments, ministries, ministerial responsibility, crises and party-state tension, but cannot create appointment, delegation, law, budget authority, confidential access or evidence for the current case.

## Materialized foundation

- Four fictional scenario actors and four government-leadership work surfaces.
- Persistent work object: `regjeringssak_prioritering_faggrunnlag_samordning_beslutning_iverksetting_og_oppfolging` with explicit title, authority, waiting, handoff and bounded rework.
- Fifteen source mails across all nine canonical mail types: 4 job, 4 people and one each of conflict, story, event, micro, followup, knowledge and consequence.
- Sixteen-step mail plan with no generic fallback.
- Situated reputation stays audience-specific; there is no global reputation score and standing cannot grant public office or authority.
