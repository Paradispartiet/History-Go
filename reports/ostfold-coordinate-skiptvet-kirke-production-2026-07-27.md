## Identity decision

The canonical object is the standing Romanesque stone church. Sources date it to the twelfth century or about 1200; the canonical year remains 1200 but is explicitly approximate.

The 1762 fire destroyed timber and interior elements and badly damaged the south wall. The church was rebuilt around surviving medieval masonry and the base of the former west tower. The current wooden porch and sacristy are from 1887, and later interior and restoration layers belong to 1895 and 1937.

The churchyard, rectory north of the church and grave chapel from 1936 are separate components.
