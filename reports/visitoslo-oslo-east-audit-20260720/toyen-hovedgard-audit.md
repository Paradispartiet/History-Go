## Source basis

VisitOSLO describes Tøyen hovedgård as a historic manor inside Botanisk hage, with a protected main wing and a history stretching back centuries. Its current listing places the visitor destination within the Sars' gate / Monrads gate side of the garden.

Oslo byleksikon identifies Tøyen hovedgård as gnr. 128/1, Trondheimsveien 23B. The main building is first described in 1721, partly built over a 17th-century cellar, and acquired essentially its present appearance through rebuilding in 1779. The estate was transferred to the king and then to the University in 1812; the surrounding land was developed as the Botanical Garden in the 1820s. The complex is protected.

The place has a strong independent physical identity: an older manor complex that predates the Botanical Garden and later became embedded within the university and museum landscape.

## Representation decision

**Approve one canonical candidate: `toyen_hovedgard`.**

The place should represent the historic manor complex itself, not the whole Botanical Garden, the whole Natural History Museum, or the contemporary café use in part of the building.

Recommended primary category: `historie`.

Core History Go angles:

- medieval and early-modern estate history
- the Bjelke period and the manor's role as a major estate
- the surviving timber main building and historical layers in the physical structure
- the 1812 transfer to the University
- the transformation of estate land into the Botanical Garden
- preservation and adaptive institutional use

## Overlap guardrails

Keep distinct:

- `botanisk_hage` — the surrounding living garden and collection landscape
- `naturhistorisk_museum` — the broader museum institution
- `klimahuset` — a separate modern exhibition building inside the garden
- `toyen_torg` — the urban square and neighbourhood anchor

Do not create a second place for the café currently occupying part of the manor unless a separate completeness policy later treats it as an independently significant venue.
