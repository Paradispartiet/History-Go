# Ullevaal Stadion commentators — research notes

## Scope

Two Norwegian sports commentators are added as individual people files linked to `ullevaal_stadion`:

- Bjørge Lillelien
- Arne Scheie

## Relevance gate

- Bjørge Lillelien is directly anchored to the Norway–England match at Ullevaal on 9 September 1981 and the radio commentary that became part of international football culture.
- Arne Scheie commentated on well over one hundred Norway national-team matches and represents the long NRK television era in which Ullevaal home matches became shared national events.

## Data model

- One person per JSON file.
- No batch people file.
- Empty `image` and `cardImage` fields remain explicit image-pipeline follow-up work.
