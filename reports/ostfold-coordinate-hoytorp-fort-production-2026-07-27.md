## Coordinate decision

The canonical marker now follows the representative point for the named Høytorp fort area. Forsvarsbygg identifies the site as establishment `012501`, a former land fort and the main fortress in Fossumstrøket. Current official site interpretation documents a 426-decare complex with 41 buildings, a central mountain installation and several fortification and visitor components.

The legacy point was approximately 1,082 metres southwest and had no Coordinate Source Contract.

## Identity and representation

Høytorp is a multi-part fort rather than one building or visitor point. The applied area anchor represents the central fortification, camp area and present cultural and recreational destination as a coherent whole.

The same-name OSM node `987933937` is a tourism-information point approximately 122.4 metres northeast and is explicitly rejected as canonical. Reduiten, the tower guns, fortress museum, artillery museum and mountain-complex entrance are important internal components but do not replace the area anchor.

The retained 420-metre gameplay radius covers the central fort and several key visitor elements. It does not cover the complete 426-decare property and is not a legal protection, property, building or complete fortification boundary.
