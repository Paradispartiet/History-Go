## Rhythm contract

The Role World must make all five states explicit while binding them only to existing mail/work evidence:

- `waiting`: source or editorial evidence is genuinely unavailable or unresolved; uncertainty remains visible rather than being converted to false certainty.
- `handoff`: work moves between research lead, source/provenance, editor and dissemination producer with the actual question, caveat and unresolved premise intact.
- `rework`: new source, conceptual or public evidence can force argument maps, attribution, inference or dissemination copy to be revised.
- `interruption`: commissioner, deadline, access or format pressure changes sequence and attention without changing epistemic or authority boundaries.
- `delayed_consequence`: omissions or shortcuts return later as source correction, public challenge or trust cost.

## Situated reputation contract

There is no global reputation score. Standing must remain audience-specific and capable of divergence across at least these audiences:

- `research_leadership`
- `source_provenance`
- `editorial_team`
- `dissemination_production`
- `commissioner_client`
- `scholarly_peers`
- `public_audience`
- `private_relations`

A concise, commissioner-friendly conclusion can improve standing with a client while reducing trust among researchers, editors or public readers if it hides the normative premise. Holding a quotation back can hurt deadline standing while improving provenance trust. A visible correction may cost short-term status while strengthening scholarly and public credibility.

Standing can never authorize unsupported attribution, hidden normative premises, strawman argumentation, rights/access/privacy violations, a predetermined research result, or use of a historical philosopher as a fictional present-day adviser or normative answer.
