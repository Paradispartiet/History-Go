# Batch 156 sources – Alnas historiske og nåværende utløp

- OpenStreetMap way 4258487: Tenerife/Vannspeilet physical water polygon used as historical display marker.
- Oslo kommune, Middelalderparken: Vannspeilet marks Alnaelvas original outlet.
- Ruter, Middelalderparken: identifies Tenerife as the nickname of the water mirror.
- OpenStreetMap way 130106085: tunneled Alna.
- OpenStreetMap way 131984275: final open Alna outlet segment.
- OpenStreetMap node 8067892897 + coastline way 865225826: explicit current river/coastline mouth topology at Kongshavn.
- Oslo byleksikon, Alnaelva: historical Sørenga outlet and current Kongshavn tunnel outlet since 1922.

The historical water mirror is a documented marker, not an invented exact medieval mouth coordinate. The legacy History Go point and nearest/first-hit selection are not used.
