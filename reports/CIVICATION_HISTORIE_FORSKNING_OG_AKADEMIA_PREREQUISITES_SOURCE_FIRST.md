# Civication — Historie / Forskning og akademia prerequisites

This package materializes the playable prerequisite foundation for `historie_forskning_og_akademia`. It is **not Role World completion** and does not create new runtime state.

## Existing contracts preserved
- Existing `civication_role_model_v2` identity, three qualification-gated career titles and the exact two work loops remain authoritative.
- Existing authority boundary remains unchanged: the role may produce and assess historical knowledge, but may not invent sources, hide counter-evidence, present interpretation as uncontested fact or exercise public authority without mandate.
- `Doktorgradsstudent`, `Forsker` and `Seniorforsker` remain `qualification_required`. A History Go badge is learning evidence, never a degree, employment qualification, academic appointment or promotion.
- Existing five research practice stories remain the editorial basis: counter-evidence, fragmentary archives, anachronism, historiographical disagreement and premature publication.

## Nine runtime gaps to close
The canonical Career Gameplay audit currently lacks `day_one`, `people`, `places`, `mail`, `knowledge`, `consequences`, `performance`, `progression` and `exit`. Entry, quality axes, authority and economy are already complete and must not be weakened.

## Work-world foundation
Four explicitly fictional scenario actors:
- **Elin**, principal supervisor/senior researcher — can challenge research design, scope and argument within supervision; cannot dictate findings or manufacture qualification.
- **Joakim**, archivist/source steward — can clarify provenance, access and archival gaps; cannot decide the historical interpretation.
- **Sara**, peer reviewer/method peer — can surface counter-evidence, rival explanations and review demands; peer standing does not grant employment or public authority.
- **Malin**, research administrator/project interface — can enforce grant, ethics, documentation and deadline requirements within mandate; cannot purchase a conclusion or override research ethics.

Four work surfaces:
- `forskningsdesign_og_sporsmalsbord`
- `arkiv_og_kildespor`
- `analyse_og_motprovingsflate`
- `fagfelle_og_publiseringsflate`

Persistent work object: **`forskningslogg_og_manusspor`**. It preserves research question, source inventory/provenance, archive gaps, method, historiographical position, counter-evidence, uncertainty, ethics status, review/supervision comments, waiting states, handoffs, revisions and manuscript version.

## Work rhythm
`sporsmal -> kildesok -> kildekritikk -> analyse -> motproving -> waiting/venting -> handoff -> skriving -> fagfellevurdering -> revisjon/rework -> publisering -> læring`

Waiting is legitimate for source access, supervision clarification, ethics/privacy review, peer review or resource/funding clarification. New sources, counter-evidence, review criticism, ethics issues or citation corrections reopen only the affected claim/section with a new version and explicit owner.

## History Go affordance
`historie` may improve questions through source criticism, historiography, context discrimination, archival silence and competing interpretations. It cannot authenticate a source, award a degree, satisfy `qualification_required`, appoint a researcher, grant ethics approval or funding, accept a publication, assign academic rank or determine a historical finding.

## Mail plan
Fail-closed 16-step progression using all nine canonical mail types and no generic fallback. The package authors 15 source mails: 4 job, 4 people and one each for conflict, story, event, micro, followup, knowledge and consequence.

Research-specific pressure points include:
- a new source that weakens the hypothesis;
- fragmentary archives that overrepresent one actor group;
- a manuscript deadline before a central source group is checked;
- reviewer criticism that requires bounded revision;
- supervision, archival provenance, peer-review and grant/ethics interfaces;
- conclusion or funding pressure that exceeds the evidence;
- a newly opened archive changing the premise;
- a fast provenance/citation check before handoff;
- explicit peer-review rework;
- academic identity and sunk-cost pressure;
- publication aftermath and correction.

## Career and consequences
The role model gains a bounded career path: qualification-based entry and progression from doctoral work toward researcher/senior researcher roles through actual academic/employer processes, never through badge status. Exits include archive/museum, research administration, advising/teaching or other historically grounded work. Positive and negative runtime outcomes make evidence handling, correction and follow-up performance-visible without inventing tenure, appointment or public authority.
