# Criciúma coordinate-control batch 4 — religious heritage

Date: 2026-07-25

## Scope

This batch reviews the five religious-heritage and church-art records in `places_criciuma_05.json`:

- `criciuma_igreja_nossa_senhora_salete`
- `criciuma_cruz_sao_paulo_apostolo`
- `criciuma_capela_sao_bras`
- `criciuma_capela_sao_roque`
- `criciuma_capela_sao_sebastiao`

## Results

### Igreja Matriz Nossa Senhora da Salete

The municipal heritage page resolves the identity and address as Rua General Osvaldo Pinto da Veiga 510, Próspera. It also distinguishes the wooden church begun in 1950 from the present building designed in 1956 and completed in 1960. The record remains `needs_source` until an exact building footprint, entrance or geocoded source object is captured.

### Cruz da Igreja Católica São Paulo Apóstolo

The municipality resolves Rua Madre Teresa Michel 151, Michel, and explicitly states that Jussara Guimarães' eight-metre cross is inside the church. This is therefore an interior-artwork record, not a free-standing outdoor monument. The marker remains blocked pending host-building geometry and an explicit co-located interior coordinate role.

### Capela de São Brás

The municipality resolves Avenida Universitária, Mãe Luzia, postcode 88808-500, and connects the chapel to the historic 3ª Linha/Sangão community. No street number or exact chapel geometry is exposed. The unsupported foundation year was normalized from 1940 to an approximate 1900 marker for the officially described early-twentieth-century construction.

### Capela de São Roque

The municipality resolves Rua Luiz José Mariano 405, São Roque, and distinguishes the 1914 first chapel, the current 1949 building and Pedro Cechet's 1951 paintings. The record remains blocked pending exact building geometry or a stable source object.

### Capela São Sebastião

The municipality resolves the identity, Morro Albino locality, 1919 construction and community-built heritage history, but publishes no address in its `Como chegar?` field. The year was corrected from the unsupported 1940 value to 1919. The foundation marker remains blocked pending an official address, cadastral object or exact building geometry.
