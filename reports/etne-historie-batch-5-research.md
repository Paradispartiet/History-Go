# Etne historie batch 5 — research

## Scope and duplicate audit

The current `main` branch was searched before file creation for the selected IDs, names and relevant variants. No existing canonical place records were found for:

- `varhaug_nervik`
- `nesjarhaugen_byrkjenes`
- `gjerdesvagen_jernvinne`
- `grindheim_jernvinne`
- `keisarhaugen_frette`

This batch deliberately shifts away from large grave fields. It adds two archaeological production sites and three individually named monuments with distinct preservation histories.

## Varhaug gravrøys

The Askeladden/B2FIND record `Nervik - Fetts fk. 1, Varhaug` describes a large dry-stone cairn on top of Varhaug, a rocky hill facing the fjord south of Norevågen. Per Fett recorded it at roughly 13 by 10 metres and up to one metre high.

Coordinate: `59.67001, 5.78581`, the mapped topographic summit Varhaug in Etne. This is a concrete named landform anchor and must not be confused with Varhaug in Hå/Jæren.

Sources: Vestland fylkeskommune / Askeladden via B2FIND; mapped Varhaug hill data.

## Nesjarhaugen

The Askeladden/B2FIND record `Byrkjenes - Fetts fk. 4, Nesjarhaugen` describes a stone ring approximately five metres in diameter, now partly overgrown and only clearly visible along parts of its edge. The record states that it was said to have been damaged by German forces during the occupation and later further disturbed.

Coordinate: `59.65353, 5.79886`, representative Børjenes/Byrkjenes farm anchor. The open metadata view exposes the site description but not a direct point coordinate, so the record uses a broad radius and explicitly avoids claiming point-level precision.

Sources: Vestland fylkeskommune / Askeladden via B2FIND; mapped Børjenes/Byrkjenes locality data.

## Jernvinneanlegget i Gjerdesvågen

The Askeladden/B2FIND record `Gjerde - Jernvinneanlegg, Gjerdesvågen` states that a test pit exposed remains of an iron-production site as a charcoal-rich layer containing slag and iron; some slag retained burnt clay lining.

Coordinate: `59.6443, 5.92245`, representative Gjerdesvågen/Gjerdsvik shoreline-area anchor. The accessible metadata describes the archaeological site but does not expose a direct coordinate in the indexed search view. The marker therefore uses a broad radius and must not be presented as exact.

Sources: Vestland fylkeskommune / Askeladden via B2FIND; mapped Gjerdsvik locality data.

## Jernvinna på Grindheim

The Askeladden/B2FIND record `Grindheim - Jernvinne` describes a structure discovered about one metre below the surface during excavation of a water-pipe trench. Large stone slabs were arranged in a construction. The indexed description does not provide enough evidence to invent slag finds, an oven plan, or a secure date.

Coordinate: `59.66864, 6.00532`, representative Grindheim farm anchor. The archaeological structure itself is underground and the accessible search metadata does not expose a direct point coordinate. The broad radius and place text preserve this uncertainty.

Sources: Vestland fylkeskommune / Askeladden via B2FIND; mapped Grindheim farm data.

## Keisarhaugen på Frette

The Askeladden/B2FIND record `Frette - Fetts fk. 2, Keisarhaugen` describes `Keisarhaugen` or `Keisarholo` as a mound roughly six to eight metres in diameter and about one metre high. It had previously been dug into; a local account says later diggers struck a stone slab.

Coordinate: `59.72693, 6.16176`, representative Frette locality anchor. The accessible metadata describes the named mound but does not expose a direct point coordinate. The record therefore uses a broad radius and does not present the marker as exact.

Sources: Vestland fylkeskommune / Askeladden via B2FIND; GeoNames-derived Frette locality mapping.

## Editorial safeguards

- `varhaug_nervik` is the only place in this batch with a concrete named topographic point anchor.
- The other four records use explicit representative locality/farm anchors because the accessible open metadata does not expose direct point coordinates.
- Do not transform representative anchors into claims of exact archaeological positioning.
- Do not assign secure dates to the three burial monuments without excavation evidence.
- Keep the two iron-production records distinct: Gjerdesvågen has documented charcoal, slag, iron and burnt clay lining; Grindheim is described in the accessible record as a buried stone-built structure registered as iron production.
