## Identity resolution

The canonical object is the three-lock Ørje complex between Rødenessjøen and Øymarksjøen. It is not Ørje town centre, the canal museum alone, Ørje Brug, the power station, the canal bridge or a centroid for the complete Halden Canal.

Marker municipality documents three locks with approximately ten metres total lift. They remain hand-operated and opened in 1860. The associated lock-master and lock-assistant residences, the Engebret Soot memorial, Ørje Brug and the canal museum form the surrounding technical and industrial heritage environment.

The same entity is associated with Kulturminne ID `241336`.

The canal museum is mapped separately as OSM way `506492465`, approximately 66.4 metres from the lock anchor. It is retained as a secondary interpretation anchor rather than replacing the physical locks.

Riksantikvaren and NVE define Ørje as one of three lock complexes in the larger Halden Canal system. The complete canal extends approximately 80 kilometres and cannot be represented by one gameplay circle.

## Radius decision

The existing 420 m gameplay radius is retained. It covers the lock chambers, residences, parts of Ørje Brug and the canal museum.

The radius is explicitly not:

- the exact lock or technical-installation geometry
- a legal protection boundary
- the waterway or property boundary
- the complete Halden Canal system
