## Identity and representation

The combined record covers Moss Ironworks and the political events of August 1814. Konventionsgården is the appropriate physical anchor because it was the ironworks' main and administration building and the place where the Convention of Moss was signed.

The wider ironworks community included Mossefossen, production zones, workers' housing and later Peterson industrial facilities. These are essential historical context but do not form one captured canonical geometry.

The retained 360-metre gameplay radius covers Konventionsgården and part of the surrounding Verket environment. It is not a building polygon, legal protection boundary, property line or complete ironworks extent.

## Rejected candidates

- Former Moss Ironworks area: wider industrial landscape without one captured geometry.
- Mossefossen: separate natural and power-history site.
- Workers' housing: separate built environment within the former ironworks community.
- Legacy coordinate: approximately 413.1 metres southwest and not tied to a documented object.
