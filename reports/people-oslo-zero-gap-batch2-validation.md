## Research gate

- Wessels plass received its current name in 1891 after Johan Herman Wessel.
- Egertorget is named after brothers Herman and Thorvald Eger, who operated Egers brewery from Karl Johans gate 20.
- Grev Wedels plass is named after Herman Wedel Jarlsberg.
- Kampen kirke and Sofienberg kirke were both built from Jacob Wilhelm Nordan's designs.
- The canonical Østbanestasjonen place represents Georg Bull's preserved 1882 building. Heinrich Schirmer and Wilhelm von Hanno are not added because they designed the smaller 1854 predecessor, not the canonical 1882 station building.
