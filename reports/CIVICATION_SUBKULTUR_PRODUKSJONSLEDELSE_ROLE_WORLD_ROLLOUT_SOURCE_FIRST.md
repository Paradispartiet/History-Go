## Editorial uniqueness
This world is authored around live operational production leadership: command line and cue/version control, qualified staffing, shifts and recovery, technical stop/retest/release, venue and audience flow, artist requests, real-time escalation, near-miss learning and debrief. It is not a renamed copy of Produksjon og prosjekt.

## Situated standing
There is no global reputation score. Seven audiences can read the same operational decision differently, while standing never grants technical release, HMS/safety, employer, medical/legal, rights, hiring or disciplinary authority.

## History Go

## Cross-role boundary
