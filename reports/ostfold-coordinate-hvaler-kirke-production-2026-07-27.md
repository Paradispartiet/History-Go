## Identity and representation

The record represents the Romanesque medieval stone church at Svanekilveien 11. The churchyard, prestegård, Ørekroken and broader maritime cultural landscape remain associated context rather than coordinate substitutes.

The previous wording that the church was probably built before 1100 was stronger than the retrieved evidence. Structured and secondary sources support a cautious formulation around 1100 or in the early twelfth century. Older settlement remains found beneath the church during the 1953–1955 restoration document earlier occupation, but do not by themselves date the present church building.

The retained 240-metre gameplay radius covers the church, churchyard and part of the immediate maritime church-site environment. It is not the building polygon, cemetery boundary, archaeological locality, legal protection area or property boundary.
