## Identity decision

The canonical place represents Norderhov old rectory's main building, now occupied by Ringerikes Museum. The museum and rectory building share one physical object and can therefore use one canonical anchor.

The following remain separate components:

- Norderhov medieval church and active churchyard;
- Munkestua;
- the wider rectory farm and agricultural property;
- current museum exhibitions and collection functions.

## Chronology decision

`year: 1716` remains because the canonical identity is explicitly event-oriented. It refers to the Battle of Norderhov and the memory tradition concerning Anna Colbjørnsdatter. It is not the construction year of the rectory.

The standing building has several distinct layers:

- Svenskestua's ground floor: 1635;
- 1716 event and memory layer;
- north wing: 1729;
- south-wing second floor: 1745;
- new roof: 1822;
- Asbjørnsen and Moe meeting: 1826;
- museum reuse from the late 1950s.

The account of Anna Colbjørnsdatter must retain source-critical room. The event association is central and institutionally documented, but later heroic narration should not be treated as a fully uncontested transcript.
