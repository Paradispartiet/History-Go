# Ullevaal Stadion NFF leaders — research notes

## Scope

Five Norwegian football leaders are added as individual people files linked to `ullevaal_stadion`:

- Per Ravn Omdal
- Karen Espelund
- Sondre Kåfjord
- Yngve Hallén
- Lise Klaveness

## Relevance gate

These are not loose football associations. Ullevaal Stadion is owned by Norges Fotballforbund and houses the federation's national leadership and administration. Each person held a central NFF role with Ullevaal as the institutional base.

## Historical anchors

- Per Ravn Omdal: NFF president in two periods; later UEFA vice-president and NFF honorary president.
- Karen Espelund: NFF director, vice-president and general secretary; major figure in women's football administration.
- Sondre Kåfjord: NFF president from 2004 to 2010.
- Yngve Hallén: NFF president from 2010 to 2016.
- Lise Klaveness: 73 senior internationals for Norway, later elite-football director and elected NFF president in 2022.

## Data model

- One person per JSON file.
