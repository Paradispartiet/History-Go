# Bislett Stadion people batch 8 — research notes

## Batch logic

Batch 8 corrects the balance after several modern/international Bislett Games batches by adding historical Norwegian speed-skating anchors.

The batch deliberately prioritizes people with direct Bislett Stadion or Oslo/Bislett championship connections, not general Norwegian sports fame.

## Added in this batch

- `roald_larsen`
- `ivar_ballangrud`
- `per_ivar_moe`
- `magne_thomassen`
- `roar_gronvold`

## Duplicate gate

Repository searches before creation found no active matches for the five IDs.

## Notes on source strength

- `roald_larsen`, `magne_thomassen`, and `roar_gronvold` have explicit event pages naming Bislett Stadion as the championship venue.
- `ivar_ballangrud` and `per_ivar_moe` are historical Norwegian allround anchors tied to Oslo/Bislett's major championship era; their entries should be retained only if the project accepts Oslo/Bislett-era championship anchoring as sufficient for historical people-of-place coverage.
