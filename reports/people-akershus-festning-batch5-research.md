# People of Places — Akershus festning batch 5 research

## Scope

Batch 5 is a selective occupation and postwar legal-purge pass for `akershus_festning`.

The place gate requires direct documented physical relation to Akershus itself through imprisonment or execution. General association with occupation history, Nasjonal Samling, resistance history or the postwar legal purge is not sufficient.

## Canonical audit

Repository searches were run for proposed IDs and full-name variants before files were created.

Accepted new canonical IDs:

- `ragnar_skancke`
- `albert_viljam_hagelin`
- `siegfried_fehmer`
- `einar_donnum`
- `reidar_haaland`

No existing canonical records were found for these IDs.

`vidkun_quisling` already exists as a canonical person and was not duplicated.

## Accepted candidates

### Ragnar Skancke

**Decision:** accept.

Store norske leksikon gives his death place as Akershus festning and documents that he was executed on 28 August 1948. He was the last person executed following a court sentence in Norway.

The relation is direct and historically significant: Akershus was his execution site and the event marks the endpoint of judicial executions in Norway.

Sources:
- https://snl.no/Ragnar_Skancke
- https://www.ntnu.no/blogger/ub-spesialsamlinger/2017/03/

### Albert Viljam Hagelin

**Decision:** accept.

Hagelin was one of the most senior political figures in the NS regime and was executed at Akershus festning on 25 May 1946 after a death sentence in the legal purge.

He also participated in the Statsakt held at Akershus on 1 February 1942, giving him an additional direct physical relation to the site. The execution alone is sufficient for the place gate.

Sources:
- https://snl.no/Albert_Viljam_Hagelin
- https://lokalhistoriewiki.no/wiki/Albert_Viljam_Hagelin
- https://snl.no/Innenriksdepartementet_-_1940%E2%80%931945

### Siegfried Fehmer

**Decision:** accept.

Store norske leksikon documents Fehmer as a central Gestapo and counterintelligence officer in occupied Norway, shows him imprisoned in a cell at Akershus after the war and gives his death place as Akershus festning. He was executed there on 16 March 1948.

The relation is unusually strong because Akershus was both a documented place of imprisonment and his execution site.

Source:
- https://snl.no/Siegfried_Fehmer

### Einar Dønnum

**Decision:** accept.

Store norske leksikon identifies Dønnum as a Statspolitiet leader notorious for torture and gives his death place as Akershus festning. He was executed there on 22 April 1947 following his death sentence in the postwar legal purge.

The relation is direct through execution at the fortress.

Sources:
- https://snl.no/Einar_D%C3%B8nnum
- https://lokalhistoriewiki.no/wiki/Einar_D%C3%B8nnum_%281897%E2%80%931947%29

### Reidar Haaland

**Decision:** accept.

Haaland worked for the German security police and was convicted of treason and serious abuse during interrogations. He was executed at Akershus festning on 17 August 1945.

His execution was the first carried out in the postwar Norwegian legal purge and the first full execution of a death sentence in Norway since 1876, making the site relation both direct and historically significant.

Sources:
- https://lokalhistoriewiki.no/wiki/Reidar_Haaland
- https://www.aftenbladet.no/magasin/i/e5g9g/historien-om-reidar-haaland

## Existing canonical person not duplicated

### Vidkun Quisling

Quisling was executed at Akershus on 24 October 1945 and is one of the site's most important postwar execution relations. He already exists as canonical `vidkun_quisling`, however, and is therefore not recreated in this batch.

Source:
- https://snl.no/Akershus_slott_og_festning

## Batch decision

Batch 5 adds five historically significant people with direct documented physical relations to Akershus festning through imprisonment or execution.

The batch deliberately does not add people merely because they belonged to NS, Gestapo, Statspolitiet, the resistance movement or the postwar legal purge.
