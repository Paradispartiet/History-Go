## Identity decision

The canonical place represents **the whole island of Isegran as one integrated cultural environment**. This is necessary because the place record combines:

- the strategic island position where Glomma divides;
- fortifications and the tower;
- the former naval harbour;
- archaeological and museum activity;
- Bjarne Aas' boatyard history;
- the museum harbour and historic vessels; and
- the current regional vessel-preservation centre.

## Chronology correction

The retained `year: 1287` is carefully limited to the first known written reference and the event associated with Alv Erlingsson and Hallkjell Krøkedans.

It does **not** mean that:

- a surviving fort was built in 1287;
- the current tower is medieval; or
- the traditionally claimed medieval castle has been archaeologically proven.

Fredrikstad municipality states that no archaeological evidence has yet confirmed the alleged medieval castle. The documented military layers primarily belong to the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries.

The visible cylindrical tower wall was reconstructed in 1984–1988 using historical images and archaeological interpretation. The tower was restored and opened as a viewpoint in 2025.

## Maritime continuity

Isegran's maritime history includes its former role as a naval harbour, later shipyard and boatbuilding activity, a museum harbour and today's active vessel-preservation environment. The current centre began as Maritime Center Fredrikstad in 2000 and received status as a regional vessel-preservation centre in 2023.

This continuity is why neither the fort point nor the workshop address alone is sufficient as the canonical map anchor.

## Access and safety

The island contains public outdoor areas, paths and arrival points, but not every component is freely accessible at every time. History Go tasks must respect:

- workshop and boatyard operations;
- vessel and mooring safety;
- event closures and temporary installations;
- guided-access requirements;
- fences, signs and staff instructions; and
- river-edge and quay safety.
