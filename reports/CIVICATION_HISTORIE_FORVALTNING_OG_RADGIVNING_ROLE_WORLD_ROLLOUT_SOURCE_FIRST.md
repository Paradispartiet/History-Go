## Sociological problem

Forvaltnings- og rådgivningsarbeid is not only a technical sequence from problem to recommendation. It is a social system in which seniority, proximity to a commissioner, legal confidence, documentation discipline, stakeholder pressure, deadline pressure, institutional loyalty and career ambition can influence which uncertainties become visible and which alternatives feel speakable.

The Role World therefore asks: **How does an adviser produce useful, traceable decision support when different audiences reward speed, certainty, loyalty, procedural safety, responsiveness and professional independence differently — without turning those social judgments into legal authority or factual truth?**

A recommendation can improve standing with a commissioner while weakening trust among quality peers. Escalating a legal-basis question can frustrate a deadline owner while strengthening confidence among mandate stewards. Preserving an uncomfortable hearing objection can create short-term friction while improving long-term traceability. These judgments are deliberately situational and may diverge.

There is no global reputation score.

## Situated standing audiences

The rollout uses eight independent audiences:

1. `senior_advisers_and_quality_leads` — quality of reasoning, visible alternatives, calibrated certainty and willingness to correct.
2. `legal_authority_and_mandate_stewards` — explicit mandate, legal-basis uncertainty, delegation and correct escalation.
3. `documentation_and_records_stewards` — provenance, journal traceability, versioning, handoff and visible knowledge gaps.
4. `commissioners_and_decision_owners` — usefulness, timeliness, scope control and whether advice answers the actual decision need without pretending to be the decision.
5. `affected_stakeholders_and_hearing_voices` — whether material objections, affected perspectives and consequences remain visible rather than being reduced to noise.
6. `peer_advisers_and_cross_function_partners` — professional independence, fair work distribution, correction, bounded rework and usable handoffs.
7. `institutional_leaders_and_future_employers` — long-run judgment, reliability under pressure, correction history and ability to distinguish loyalty from compliance.
8. `private_relations` — availability, confidentiality, recovery and whether institutional conflict is carried home as identity or hidden case material.

Each audience owns a distinct standing axis. No audience can grant legal basis, delegation, a formal decision, source authentication, appointment, budget authority or truth. `direct` remains an employment-offer policy only.

## Editorial uniqueness

This Role World is not a renamed research world and not a copy of the generic city adviser world. Its distinctive pressure is **the boundary between advice and decision** inside a historically informed administrative practice:

- a commissioner may want a clean answer before the legal basis is clear;
- archived precedent may be informative without being binding;
- a documented objection can be institutionally inconvenient but decision-relevant;
- journal completeness is not source authentication;
- senior confidence is not delegated authority;
- a direct career offer is not a mandate to decide;
- a high-status adviser can still be wrong, out of scope or dependent on another authority holder.

The season must repeatedly force the player to distinguish documented fact, historical context, professional assessment, recommendation and formal decision.

## History Go affordance

History Go can improve questions about historical precedent, institutional memory, changing administrative categories, archival provenance, source criticism, historiography, analogy and missing voices. It may help the adviser ask whether an old practice is genuinely comparable, whether a source was produced for another purpose, whose perspective is absent and whether an institutional narrative has become a hidden premise.

History Go and the History Badge **cannot** authenticate a source, create legal basis, give delegated authority, decide a case, issue a formal decision, replace the actual decision owner, turn precedent into binding law, appoint the player, grant budget authority or make a recommendation correct because it is historically plausible.
