## Research gate

- Canonical `tullin` is anchored at Tullinløkka, which is named after Claus Tullin. Christian Braunmann Tullin is rejected because Tullins gate, not Tullinløkka, is named after the poet.
- Alexander Kiellands plass received its name in 1914 after Alexander Lange Kielland.
- Hønse-Lovisas hus takes its name from the literary figure in Oskar Braaten's `Ungen`.
- Future Library is Katie Paterson's 100-year public artwork in Nordmarka.
- Roseslottet identifies Vebjørn Sand and Eimund Sand as its two creators.
- Frammuseet identifies Fram as its central physical object: Nansen, Sverdrup and Amundsen led the three Fram expeditions, Colin Archer designed and built the ship, and Sverdrup led the preservation campaign that made the museum possible.
