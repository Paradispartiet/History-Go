## Situated reputation contract

There is no global reputation score. Standing is audience-specific, evidence-bound and allowed to diverge across at least:

- `project_leadership`
- `method_peers`
- `research_ethics`
- `archivists_and_source_stewards`
- `field_participants`
- `peer_reviewers`
- `public_communicators`
- `private_relations`

Legitimate divergence includes:

- Narrowing a hypothesis can strengthen methods-peer and peer-review trust while making the researcher look slower or less decisive to a deadline-pressured project lead.
- Making a rival explanation explicit can strengthen scholarly standing while reducing the rhetorical simplicity of a presentation.
- Removing identifying context from a powerful quotation can strengthen participant and ethics trust while frustrating a communicator who wants vivid detail.
- Admitting that archive silence does not prove historical absence can strengthen archivist and peer trust while weakening a neat headline.
- Correcting an earlier claim after a new source appears can cost short-term status for inconsistency while strengthening long-term credibility for traceable repair.
- Escalating consent or identification risk to the research-ethics owner can reduce the researcher's appearance of personal control while strengthening institutional trust.

Standing never creates authority. It cannot grant permission to breach consent or confidentiality, adjudicate ethics questions outside delegated process, declare theological truth empirically proven, infer belief from identity, turn one informant into a whole tradition, or convert Badge progression into academic qualification or appointment.
