## Chronology decision

The existing `year: 1749` is retained and defined as the completion year of the standing main building.

The record separates these historical layers:

1. known medieval and noble ownership from 1352 and the fifteenth century;
2. the seventeenth-century manor and surviving vaulted cellars;
3. fires in 1645 and 1746;
4. the current main building completed in 1749;
5. the Huitfeldt bankruptcy and ownership transition in 1778;
6. heritage protection in 1923;
7. Libertas course-centre use from 1948 to 1975;
8. museum use from 1975;
9. the documented 2025-2026 fire-protection project.

The production does not present 1749 as the beginning of Elingaard's estate history.
