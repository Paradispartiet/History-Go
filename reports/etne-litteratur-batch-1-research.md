# Etne litteratur batch 1 — research

## Scope

Three concrete literary anchors were selected after auditing current `main` for IDs, names and variants:

- `olav_vik_garden_osnes`
- `ingvar_moe_byste_etne`
- `gurine_johan_ebnes_minde`

No existing canonical place records were found for these candidates.

## 1. Olav Vik-garden på Osnes

**Decision:** include.

The strongest literary place in the batch. Friluftsrådet Vest states that the Olav Vik foundation area on Osnes is where the poet had his farm and found inspiration for his writing. Brønnøysundregistrene lists Olav Vik Stiftelsen Osnes at Olav Viks veg 28 and classifies it as operation of historical sites and monuments. Etne Sogelag documents Olav Vik's poetry collections and local literary legacy.

Sources:

- https://www.frivest.no/lokalt/etne/osnes
- https://virksomhet.brreg.no/nb/oppslag/enheter/971354550
- https://www.etne-sogelag.no/enkeltboker/det-liv-som-gror-or-draumen-av-olav-vik
- https://www.etne.kommune.no/kultur-og-fritid/idrett-og-friluftsliv/friluftsliv/

**Coordinate gate:** resolve `Olav Viks veg 28, 5590 Etne` through Kartverket/Geonorge. The place record represents the literary farm/property, not all of the wider Osnes recreation area.

## 2. Ingvar Moe-bysten i Etne

**Decision:** include.

Store norske leksikon describes Ingvar Moe as an important Norwegian author whose writing repeatedly engaged with his home village Etne. The Norwegian children's and youth authors' association documented that a bust of Moe was unveiled in 2006 and placed by the quay in Etne, near the place where he was born.

Sources:

- https://snl.no/Ingvar_Moe
- https://www.etne.kommune.no/kultur-og-fritid/historiske-namn-og-boker/ingvar-moe/
- https://www.nbuforfattere.no/2006/12/02/ingvar-moe-p-sokkel/
- https://www.nbuforfattere.no/2006/12/11/ingvar-moe-hedret/

**Coordinate gate:** the source documents the bust as being by the Etne quay but does not provide an exact machine-readable monument coordinate. The record therefore uses the already verified Sjoarvegen 2/quayfront anchor as a disclosed representative point. It must not be described as centimetre-precise.

## 3. Boksamlinga Gurine og Johan Ebnes Minde

**Decision:** include with representative locality anchor.

Etne kommune documents the testamentary creation of a book collection for Ebne school district, covering Ebne, Sandvik and Lovik. The collection was to follow public-library principles and a 1953 supplementary testament explicitly required it to remain separate from the municipal library in Skånevik. The municipality still lists the collection as an organisation.

Sources:

- https://www.etne.kommune.no/kultur-og-fritid/legat/ebne-skulekrins-legatmidlar/
- https://www.etne.kommune.no/kultur-og-fritid/lag-og-organisasjonar/

**Coordinate gate:** open sources do not identify a reliable current collection building. The integration may use a broad representative Ebne locality/community anchor, but the record must explicitly state that this is not a claimed exact present-day library location.

## Exclusions

- Etne bibliotek, Etne branch: not split into a new literary place because its functions are physically consolidated in the already canonical `skakke_kultursenter_etne` complex.
- Skånevik library: not added without a distinct place-level story and coordinate audit strong enough to justify splitting it from existing Skånevik culture facilities.
- Etne Sogelag and Etne Mållag: important literary/local-history organisations, but no distinct permanent physical place was established for this batch.

## Source priority

Primary/public sources were preferred: Etne kommune, Brønnøysundregistrene, Friluftsrådet Vest and Store norske leksikon. The author-organisation source was used specifically for the documented placement and unveiling of the Ingvar Moe bust.
