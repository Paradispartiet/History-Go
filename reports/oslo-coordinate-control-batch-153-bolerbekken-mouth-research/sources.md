# Batch 153 research sources

- Østensjøvannets Venner documents Bølerbekken as a major tributary and states that it flows into Østensjøvannet after passing the bridge at Valborgs vei.
- The local visitor guide recommends the south end and Bølerbekken outlet for bird observation, and publishes a GPS reference for the south-end bridge.
- The GPS reference is only a scope crosscheck; the research audits exact named OSM Bølerbekken geometry and endpoint topology.
- The legacy History Go south-zone coordinate is not used.
