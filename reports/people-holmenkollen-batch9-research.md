# Holmenkollen People of Place — batch 9 research

Date: 2026-07-18
Place: `holmenkollen_nasjonalanlegg`
Category: `sport`

## Selection principle

Batch 9 uses direct documented individual competition victories in Holmenkollen as the place gate. General skiing fame, Oslo association or Holmenkollmedaljen alone is not sufficient.

## Canonical audit

Fresh repository searches were run before file creation for proposed IDs, full names and relevant transliteration/name variants. No existing canonical people records were found for:

- `marjatta_kajosmaa`
- `raisa_smetanina`
- `alevtina_koltsjina`
- `galina_kulakova`
- `hallgeir_brenden`

The Alevtina audit also checked the common `Kolchina` transliteration.

## Selected candidates

### Marjatta Kajosmaa

Direct Holmenkollen gate: seven individual victories from 1969 through 1973 — three on 5 km and four on 10 km. Store norske leksikon identifies this as the women's Holmenkollen victory record.

### Raisa Smetanina

Direct Holmenkollen gate used in the canonical record:

- 5 km winner in 1975
- 10 km winner in 1975
- 5 km winner in 1979
- world champion on 20 km in Holmenkollen in 1982

Source note: the dedicated SNL biography contains a conflicting sentence that assigns her a 10 km Holmenkollen victory in 1979. The year-by-year SNL winner table instead lists Galina Kulakova as the 10 km winner and Smetanina as the 5 km winner. Olympedia independently describes Smetanina's third Holmenkollen victory as the 5 km in 1979. The batch therefore follows the detailed result table and the independent corroboration.

### Alevtina Koltsjina

Direct Holmenkollen gate: three consecutive 10 km victories in 1961, 1962 and 1963, plus victory in the 5 km World Championship race in Holmenkollen in 1966.

### Galina Kulakova

Direct Holmenkollen gate: 10 km victories in 1970 and 1979.

### Hallgeir Brenden

Direct Holmenkollen gate: 18 km victory in 1952 and 15 km victories in 1956 and 1963.
