# Holmenkollen People of Place — batch 7 research

Date: 2026-07-18
Place: `holmenkollen_nasjonalanlegg`
Category: `sport`

## Selection principle

Batch 7 uses direct documented victory in the Holmenkollen ski-jumping competition as the place gate. General ski fame, Holmenkollmedaljen alone, or broad Oslo association is not sufficient.
