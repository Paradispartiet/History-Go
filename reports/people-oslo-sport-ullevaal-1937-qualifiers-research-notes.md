# Ullevaal Stadion 1937 World Cup qualifiers — research notes

## Scope

Five historic Norway profiles are added as individual people files linked directly to `ullevaal_stadion`:

- Reidar Kvammen
- Alf Martinsen
- Arne Brustad
- Nils Eriksen
- Asbjørn Halvorsen

## Direct Ullevaal anchor

Norway beat Ireland 3–2 at Ullevaal Stadion on 10 October 1937 in qualification for the 1938 World Cup.

- Reidar Kvammen scored Norway's first two goals.
- Alf Martinsen scored the winning goal.
- Arne Brustad played in the Norwegian forward line.
- Nils Eriksen captained Norway.
- Asbjørn Halvorsen led the team as national coach.

The home victory was followed by a 3–3 draw in Dublin and formed the decisive qualification sequence that sent Norway to its first World Cup finals.

## Biographical corroboration

- Reidar Kvammen: 51 senior internationals, 17 goals, first Norwegian to reach 50 caps, 1936 Olympic bronze medallist and 1938 World Cup player.
- Alf Martinsen: 25 senior internationals, 10 goals, 1936 Olympic bronze medallist and member of the 1938 World Cup squad.
- Arne Brustad: 33 senior internationals and 17 goals; scored five times at the 1936 Olympics and Norway's goal against Italy at the 1938 World Cup.
- Nils Eriksen: 47 senior internationals, 1936 Olympic bronze medallist and 1938 World Cup player.
- Asbjørn Halvorsen: Norway national coach from 1936 to 1940, architect of the Olympic bronze team and the first World Cup qualification; later NFF secretary general.
