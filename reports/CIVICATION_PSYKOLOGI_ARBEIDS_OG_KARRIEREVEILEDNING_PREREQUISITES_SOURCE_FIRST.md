# Arbeids- og karriereveiledning — source-first prerequisites

This change establishes the missing playable career foundation for `psykologi/psykologi_arbeids_og_karriereveiledning`; it is not Role World completion. The existing badge routing, evidence registry, authority boundary, four practice stories and Life Story are preserved.

The persistent object is one consent-bounded choice and transition plan. It carries the seeker's own goal, evidence, alternatives, consent scope, waiting points, next action, owner and review date without turning health history or an interest tool into a diagnosis. Four fictional scenario actors and four organizational work surfaces make goal ownership, mapping, employer dialogue and public-service handoff concrete.

Fifteen canonical scenes cover all nine mail types. They form a repeatable rhythm of clarification, waiting for the seeker's or partner's response, bounded handoff, follow-up and rework when the plan does not survive contact with everyday life. History Go is used to ask how guidance institutions, testing traditions and labor-market categories shape what appears possible; historical framing never determines individual fit or grants clinical authority.

Cross-role remains `candidate_when_shared_work_is_real`. No shared runtime object is invented. Audience-bounded Role World standing remains the only authored gap after this prerequisite.
