## Identity decision

The canonical place represents **Sarpefossen itself**, the waterfall in Glomma. Kartverket identifies the object as a waterfall with place number `447090`, coordinate `59.27634, 11.13123`, and the approved prioritised spelling `Sarpefossen`.

`Sarpsfossen` and `Sarpen` remain useful alternative names in descriptions and search terms, but the canonical display name follows SSR.

The place does not represent:

- the Borregaard industrial complex;
- Hafslund, Sarp or Borregaard power stations;
- the road or railway bridges;
- one riverbank locality; or
- the public viewpoint.

## Historical framing

Sarpsborg municipality describes how Glomma broke through the Raet moraine approximately 4,000 years ago. The canonical `year: 1016` is retained as the historical layer for the foundation of Borg beside the waterfall according to the saga tradition; it is explicitly not presented as the waterfall's geological age.

The municipality and Store norske leksikon document the later use of the fall for mills, sawmills, industry and hydroelectric production. SNL describes a utilised head of 20.5 metres and three power stations associated with the waterfall.
