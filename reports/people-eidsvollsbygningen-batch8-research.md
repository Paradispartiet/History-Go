# People of Places — Eidsvollsbygningen batch 8 research

## Scope and significance threshold

Batch 8 applies a stricter selection rule than earlier expansion batches. Physical participation in the Riksforsamlingen at Eidsvoll in 1814 remains mandatory, but participation alone is no longer sufficient.

A candidate must also have at least one strong significance signal:

- a clearly consequential role in the constitutional or institutional work at Eidsvoll
- leadership of an important committee or of the assembly itself
- a distinctive and historically important position in a major constitutional debate
- or substantial later national political significance that makes the person more than a marginal assembly participant

Candidates whose main qualification is merely attendance, a minor intervention, passive faction membership, or local prominence are excluded from this batch.

The initial shortlist also considered Palle Rømer Fleischer and Gustav Peter Blom. Both were excluded from batch 8 because their roles in the Riksforsamlingen itself were comparatively passive, and stronger candidates with more direct historical significance at Eidsvoll were available.
