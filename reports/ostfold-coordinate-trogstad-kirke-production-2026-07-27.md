## Identity decision

The canonical object is the standing Romanesque stone church, usually dated to about 1250. The church retains a medieval nave and narrower chancel, including an original east window.

The 1904 project under Halfdan Berle was a major extension and interior alteration. It did not replace the entire medieval church. The west wall and porch were removed and the nave was extended with a narrower brick section.

Trøgstad bygdemuseum, the chapel, the churchyard and the rectory are related but separate components.
