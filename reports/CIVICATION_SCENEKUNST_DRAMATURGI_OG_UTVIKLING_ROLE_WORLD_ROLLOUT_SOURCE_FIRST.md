## Source-first world

The season follows one production from text version 04 through structural analysis, source uncertainty, a contested chorus, rehearsal evidence, a rights refusal, bounded rework, a leaked historical claim and final handoff. All 56 day/phase beats are unique, each is bound to exactly one existing authored mail, and every one of the 15 source mails is exercised at least three times.

Seven evolving threads keep the world relational rather than episodic:

1. Liv's text, authorship and consent.
2. Samir's rehearsal evidence and directing mandate.
3. Ingrid's source boundary, late dissent and correction.
4. Maja's delivery, workload and bounded rework.
5. The chorus conflict across divided artistic mandates.
6. A historical claim moving into public circulation and back through correction.
7. Private containment without heroic permanent availability.

Eight delayed consequences return on later days across job, relationship, psyche, livelihood, economy, reputation and narrative domains. Earlier observations and decisions remain versioned when a premise changes; rework reopens only the affected text, source, scene and production surfaces.

## Situated reputation and authority

Standing is kept separate for authors/rightsholders, director/ensemble, research/archives, production/technical, institutional leadership, communication/publics and private relations. The same choice can improve standing with one audience and weaken it with another. No audience can convert standing into copyright, usage rights, directing or authorial mandate, programming or budget authority, historical fact approval, access to confidential material, or an ungoverned transfer of responsibility.

History Go is bounded to a Johanne Dybwad/Nationaltheatret source path that sharpens questions about text, acting, institution and changing interpretation. It cannot decide the fictional production's staging, validate its historical claim, grant usage rights or create artistic authority.

The cross-role candidate remains `not_required_for_rollout`. No cross-role runtime or `shared_work_object` is materialized before a real shared work surface, version, right, owner and handoff exist.

## Editorial uniqueness

The 56 beats are not a mail permutation or a generic creative-work wrapper. They preserve concrete dramaturgical distinctions—text function versus taste, rehearsal event versus explanation, artistic choice versus source claim, rights refusal versus aesthetic advice, and production limitation versus artistic veto. The public error emerges from an earlier version boundary, the failed pause hypothesis changes later authorship work, and Maja's invisible coordination changes which rework is possible.
