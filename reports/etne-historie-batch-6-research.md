# Etne historie batch 6 — research

## Scope and duplicate audit

The current `main` branch was searched before file creation for the selected IDs and names. No existing canonical place records were found for:

- `dysjanes_rivaisen_gravroys`
- `hidlesnes_nernes_gravroys`
- `vardahaugen_lauareid`
- `stampehaug_meland`
- `hoyland_gravhaug_etne`

This batch broadens the geographic spread of Etne's prehistory, especially toward coastal and outer farm landscapes.

## Gravrøysa på Dysjanes

The Askeladden/B2FIND record `Rivåsen - Fetts fk. 1, Dysjanes` describes a cairn remnant on Dysjanes, a rocky headland in the outfield. Per Fett recorded an original diameter of about 8–9 metres; the cairn was already low and disturbed.

Coordinate: `59.65991, 5.86470`, mapped topographic point Dysjanes in Etne.

Sources: Vestland fylkeskommune / Askeladden via B2FIND; mapped Dysjanes landform data.

## Gravrøysa på Hidlesnes

The Askeladden/B2FIND record `Nernes - Fetts fk. 1, Hidlenes` describes a cairn on the outer high point of Hidlenes. It was about six metres in diameter and half a metre high, heavily disturbed, with a central depression where charcoal was reportedly found on the cairn floor.

Coordinate: `59.64636, 5.90783`, mapped topographic point Hidlesnes in Etne.

Sources: Vestland fylkeskommune / Askeladden via B2FIND; mapped Hidlesnes landform data.

## Vardahaugen på Lauareid

The Askeladden/B2FIND record `Lauareid - Fetts fk. 1, Vardahaugen` places a dry-stone cairn on the highest point of a rocky ridge about 200 metres east of the farmyard. It was recorded at about 10–11 metres in diameter and roughly half a metre high.

Coordinate: `59.72245, 6.12506`, derived representative point approximately 200 metres east of the mapped Lauareid locality anchor. The open indexed metadata does not expose a direct archaeological point coordinate, so the radius and notes preserve this limitation.

Sources: Vestland fylkeskommune / Askeladden via B2FIND; mapped Lauareid locality data.

## Stampehaug på Meland

The Askeladden/B2FIND record `Meland - Fetts fk. 2, Stampehaug` describes a mound on a natural hump close west of the river, about 70–80 metres north of the farmyard. The artificial addition was estimated at about eight metres wide and one metre high.

Coordinate: `59.63973, 5.90045`, derived representative point approximately 75 metres north of the mapped Meland farm anchor. It is not presented as an exact archaeological point.

Sources: Vestland fylkeskommune / Askeladden via B2FIND; mapped Meland farm data.

## Gravhaugen på Høyland

The Askeladden/B2FIND record `Høyland - Fetts fk. 3` describes a mound immediately in front of the driving bridge to the barn. The mound was about ten metres in diameter and 1.25 metres high, built of stone with a thin turf covering and some edge damage.

Coordinate: `59.64797, 6.00673`, representative Høyland-area anchor. The accessible metadata description is precise relative to the farm buildings, but the indexed open view does not expose a direct archaeological point coordinate.

Sources: Vestland fylkeskommune / Askeladden via B2FIND; mapped Høyland locality/bridge data.

## Editorial safeguards

- Dysjanes and Hidlesnes use concrete named topographic anchors.
- Vardahaugen and Stampehaug use derived representative anchors based on explicit historical distance directions from mapped locality/farm anchors.
- Høyland uses a representative local anchor because the direct archaeological point is not exposed in the indexed metadata.
- None of the five monuments is given a secure exact prehistoric date without excavation evidence.
- Do not describe disturbed cairns as intact monuments.
