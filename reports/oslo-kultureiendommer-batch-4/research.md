### Geitmyra gård

No canonical place was found for `Geitmyra gård`, `Geitmyra matkultursenter` or Tåsenveien 2. Oslo kommune documents the property back to 1696 and the present main building to 1755. The municipality bought the property in 1884 for Nordre gravlund, and the preserved farm buildings are now used by Geitmyra matkultursenter for barn.

Representation: `historie`, combining historic farm architecture, municipal land-use change and present food-culture reuse.

### Grønland politistasjon

No canonical place was found for `Grønland politistasjon`, `Bækkegatens politistasjon` or Tøyenbekken 5. The building opened as a police station in 1903; the preserved arrest wing was used by the German Schnellkommando during the occupation. The police moved out in 1978, and the building now houses cultural and museum activities.

Representation: `historie`, with policing, occupation and adaptive cultural reuse as separate layers.
