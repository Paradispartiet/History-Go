## Coordinate decision

The canonical marker now follows the representative point for the named and partly restored Basmo fortress ruin. The OSM object is linked to Wikidata `Q4867802`; Wikidata identifies Kulturminne ID `86068` and publishes an entity coordinate approximately 33.9 metres from the applied point. Geotagged ruin photographs provide an independent physical cross-check approximately 30.5 metres away.

The legacy point was approximately 9.36 kilometres northeast and did not represent Basmo festning.

## Identity and representation

The applied anchor represents the restored main ruin on the hill, particularly the surviving and reconstructed lower tower and cellar structures. The historical fortress was larger and included batteries, command and service buildings, barracks below the hill and a retranchement across the valley floor.

The retained 320-metre gameplay radius covers the ruin and much of the immediate approach landscape. It is not the ruin polygon, archaeological locality, legal protection area, property boundary or complete historical fortress extent.

## Rejected candidates

- Basmo farm or Basmoen locality: broader place identity rather than the fortress.
- Bus stop and information board: access and interpretation infrastructure.
- Historical batteries, barracks and retranchement: wider former components not represented by the main ruin polygon.
- Legacy coordinate: approximately 9,358.7 metres northeast and unrelated to the site.
