# Religion / Utredning og rådgivning — prerequisite source-first

## Scope lock
This package completes the existing canonical role scope `religion_utredning_og_radgivning` in place. It is prerequisite foundation work, not Role World completion, and it creates no parallel career identity.

## Canonical career policy
- **Fagkonsulent** remains `direct` with no invented qualification gate.
- **Seniorrådgiver** remains `direct` with no invented qualification gate.
- Direct entry and seniority never imply delegated decision authority, legal competence, personnel authority, budget authority or representasjonsmandat.

## Persistent work
The role now owns the persistent case object `utredningslogg_bestilling_kilder_aktorer_rettigheter_analyse_anbefaling_beslutning_og_oppfolging`. It keeps bestilling, actual decision owner, mandate, sources and proveniens, attributed actor perspectives, internal variation, rights/legal boundary, analysis, alternatives, recommendation, handoff, actual decision and follow-up separately versioned. Waiting, handoff and bounded rework are explicit.

## People and work surfaces
Four fictional scenario actors cover mandate/bestilling, sources/pluralism, legal/rights escalation and quality/handoff. Four work surfaces make the same boundaries operational. They are fictional workplace scaffolding and are not canonical historical People or public Places.

## Mail and day one
There are **15 source mails** across all nine required types and a 16-step no-fallback plan. Day one starts with one concrete bestilling and requires an actual decision owner and mandate before the first recommendation.

## History Go boundary
History Go and the Religion badge may improve historical context, chronology, comparison and questions. They do not prove today's case facts, actor mandate, legal conclusion, representasjonsmandat, delegation or authority.

## Situated reputation
The prerequisite package authors only the foundation: standing may affect trust, tone and cooperation, never evidence, access, legal competence, representation or authority. Dedicated Role World authoring remains required for the situated-reputation realism layer.

## Cross-role
`cross_role_need` remains **not_required_for_rollout**. Shared work must not be invented from thematic proximity.

## Runtime
**No new runtime.** Existing Civication scene, mail, career and History Go pipelines remain canonical.
