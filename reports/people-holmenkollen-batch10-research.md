# Holmenkollen People of Place — batch 10 research

Date: 2026-07-18
Branch: `data/people-holmenkollen-batch10-20260718`

## Scope

Five additional canonical people records for `holmenkollen_nasjonalanlegg`, selected only when there is a direct documented individual Holmenkollen competition victory.

## Selected profiles

### Toini Gustafsson (`toini_gustafsson`)
- Won the women's 10 km in Holmenkollen in 1960, 1967 and 1968.
- Received the Holmenkollen Medal in 1967.
- Primary source: Store norske leksikon, `Toini Gustafsson`.

### Klavdija Bojarskikh (`klavdija_bojarskikh`)
- Won the women's 10 km in Holmenkollen in 1965 and the 1966 World Championship 10 km in Holmenkollen.
- Won the women's 5 km in Holmenkollen in 1967.
- Store norske leksikon summarizes this as three Holmenkollen victories.
- Primary sources: Store norske leksikon, `Klavdija Sergejevna Bojarskikh` and `Holmenkollrennene - vinnere`.

### Helena Takalo (`helena_takalo`)
- Won both the women's 5 km and 10 km in Holmenkollen in 1976.
- Received the Holmenkollen Medal in 1977.
- Primary source: Store norske leksikon, `Helena Takalo`.

### Hilkka Riihivuori (`hilkka_riihivuori`)
- Won the women's 10 km in Holmenkollen in 1974 and the 5 km in 1977 under the surname Kuntola.
- Won both 5 km and 10 km in 1980 under the surname Riihivuori.
- The canonical record uses her later and widely used competition name `Hilkka Riihivuori`, while the popup explicitly preserves the Kuntola name used in the earlier result lists.
- Primary source: Store norske leksikon, `Holmenkollrennene - vinnere`.

### Jelena Välbe (`jelena_valbe`)
- Won the women's 5 km in Holmenkollen in 1991.
- Won the women's 15 km in Holmenkollen in 1992.
- Received the Holmenkollen Medal in 1992.
- Primary source: Store norske leksikon, `Jelena Välbe`.

## Place gate

Every record is anchored by one or more direct individual victories in Holmenkollen. General ski fame, Olympic or World Championship results elsewhere, medal awards alone, and broad Oslo associations are not sufficient for inclusion.
