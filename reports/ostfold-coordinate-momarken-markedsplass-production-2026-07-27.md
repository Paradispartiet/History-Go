## Identity decision

The canonical object is the modern Momarken market and event site used by Momarkedet from 1950 to 2012. The year changes from unsupported 1900 to the documented start of the modern festival in 1950.

The older market is documented from 1611 and was closed in 1830, but the sources do not establish that its exact footprint was identical to the modern event-site point. It is retained as a broader historical layer.

Momarken Travbane opened in 1920 and is a separate sports facility. The racecourse oval, motor track and Travmuseum are not used as the market-place coordinate.

## Historical layers

- 1611: older Momarken market documented
- 1820: broader area used as a military drill ground
- 1830: older market closed
- 1920: separate Momarken racecourse opened
- 1950: first modern Momarkedet
- 1950–2012: recurring market festival and Røde Kors fundraising event
- 1982: separate motor-sport track entered use
- after 2012: intermittent successor event concepts

## Access and representation limits

The marker represents the modern market and event site. It does not represent the full racecourse, motor track, stalls, depot, museum or the unresolved footprint of the older market.

- access follows races, festivals, fairs, rentals and rigging periods
- track, depot, animal, vehicle and restricted event areas require explicit access
- current barriers and safety instructions take priority
- the 300-metre radius is not a market, racecourse, motor-sport, property, event, safety or access boundary
