## Situated reputation

- 8 bounded audiences, 9 slow editorial-only axes and **no global reputation score**.
- Standing is relational: it may affect access, information flow, collaboration, attention and willingness to involve the player early.
- Standing is not evidence. Seniority, field trust, laboratory trust, analytical prestige, commissioner approval or peer attention cannot make a hypothesis true or make weak data valid.
- Audiences cover method/senior peers, field teams/local observers, laboratory quality/technical staff, data/statistical/reproducibility peers, funders/commissioners/decision users, ethics/governance, external scientific peers/publication and private relations.

## Research-specific social problem

The Role World makes the social cost of corrigible research visible. The player can lose speed, status or a clean story by preserving field deviations, stopping a laboratory series, showing sensitivity, accepting a weaker replication, resisting commissioner pressure or revising a manuscript after review. Those losses may improve situated trust in one audience while reducing it in another. None of those standings may be pooled into one reputation score or used to bypass method, data, qualification, employment, ethics or authority.

## Season and continuity

- 14 days × 4 phases = 56 unique beats.
- 7 multi-day primary relationship threads.
- 5 private aftermaths.
- 8 delayed consequences.
- The season follows design, field sampling, laboratory control, dataset versioning, ethics/habilitation, commissioner pressure, negative findings, replication, calibration drift, unit mismatch/sensitivity, peer review, publication deadline, final report and archive/correction.
- Waiting, handoff and rework remain bound to the existing persistent research contract.

## History Go boundary

History Go can surface species, places, ecological relations, natural-history context and concepts that improve the research question. It cannot provide field data, laboratory proof, research-ethics approval, a degree, `relevant_education_or_employer_qualification`, `academic_qualification_and_employment`, research employment or evidence that a hypothesis is true. Natur-Badge is learning support only.

## Authority boundary

The role may analyse data, formulate professional conclusions within competence and mandate, and report uncertainty and methodological limitations. It may not falsify qualifications or data, hide negative or uncertain findings, make political decisions, or treat the researcher role as automatic public-administration authority. Audience standing cannot change these boundaries.

## Editorial uniqueness

This world is not a copy of `historie/historie_forskning_og_akademia`, `psykologi/forsker_psykologi` or `religion/religion_forskning`. Its authored standing is specifically tied to biological/ecological field evidence, sample traceability, laboratory controls, dataset versions, replication, commissioner pressure around empirical nature findings and the private cost of correcting a research claim.
