# Ullevaal Stadion women people — research notes

## Scope

Five existing Norwegian women's national-team profiles are added as individual people files linked to `ullevaal_stadion`:

- Hege Riise
- Linda Medalen
- Marianne Pettersen
- Solveig Gulbrandsen
- Caroline Graham Hansen

## Relevance gate

Each person has a substantial Norway senior national-team career. Ullevaal Stadion is used as the national home-ground anchor for Norway's women's national-team history, home matches, qualification campaigns and public football memory.

## Historical anchors

- Hege Riise: 1995 World Cup winner and 2000 Olympic champion; 188 senior internationals.
- Linda Medalen: central scorer and 1995 World Cup winner.
- Marianne Pettersen: 1995 World Cup winner and 2000 Olympic champion.
- Solveig Gulbrandsen: 2000 Olympic champion and long-serving national-team midfielder.
- Caroline Graham Hansen: leading modern Norway international and major European club-football profile.

## Data model

- One person per JSON file.
- No batch people file.
- Empty `image` and `cardImage` fields remain explicit image-pipeline follow-up work.
