## Identity resolution

The historical factory occupied a large and changing industrial site in central Askim. The strongest surviving accessible physical anchor is the named Askimtorget building, which the site's own history identifies as former Viking Askim factory premises.

The canonical point therefore follows the reused building. The present shopping-centre function is a later layer and not the historical subject of the record. The point does not claim that the entire current building is unchanged from 1920 or that it represents the complete former factory site.

## Applied source

OpenStreetMap way `244042217` maps the Askimtorget building and links it to Wikidata `Q11959366`. Its representative point is:

- `59.58334, 11.16558`

Askimtorget documents that Viking Askim Gummivarefabrikk operated in the building and that commercial and service uses developed into the current centre after industrial activity ended. Its historical page dates the factory opening to 17 January 1920 and identifies access to power and the railway as important location factors.

Store norske leksikon documents the 1906 predecessor, the 1920 Askim company and tyre production from 1931. Lokalhistoriewiki cross-checks the footwear, tyre and technical-rubber production, approximately 2,200 employees in 1950 and closure in 1991.
