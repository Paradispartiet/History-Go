# Ullevaal Stadion coaches — research notes

## Scope

Five Norwegian national-team coaches and leaders are added as individual people files linked to `ullevaal_stadion`:

- Egil «Drillo» Olsen
- Åge Hareide
- Per-Mathias Høgmo
- Ståle Solbakken
- Nils Johan Semb

## Relevance gate

Each person has a direct senior national-team connection to Ullevaal Stadion through the role as Norway head coach, qualification home matches or a combined player/coach career on the national arena.

## Historical anchors

- Egil Olsen: Norway head coach in the World Cup qualification eras for 1994 and 1998; the 2–0 win over England at Ullevaal in 1993 is a central stadium moment.
- Åge Hareide: played in the 2–1 win over England at Ullevaal in 1981 and later coached Norway from 2003 to 2008.
- Per-Mathias Høgmo: Norway men’s head coach from 2013 to 2016; previously led the women’s national team to Olympic gold in 2000.
- Ståle Solbakken: 58 senior caps as a player and Norway head coach from 2020, linking two Ullevaal eras.
- Nils Johan Semb: Norway head coach from 1998 to 2003 and qualified the team for Euro 2000.

## Data model

- One person per JSON file.
- No batch people file.
- Empty `image` and `cardImage` fields remain explicit image-pipeline follow-up work.
