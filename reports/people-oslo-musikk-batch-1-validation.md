## Research gate

### Det Norske Teatret

Repo basis: `data/places/musikk/oslo/places_musikk.json` defines `det_norske_teatret` as a theatre and music stage in central Oslo, tied to scenekunst, musical productions and language politics.

External basis: the official Det Norske Teatret site shows an active theatre front page with current programme/presentation and lists the visiting address as `Kristian IVs g. 8, 0164 OSLO`.

Decision: safe to add `det_norske_teatret_musikkteatermiljoet` as a collective music-theatre / ensemble anchor.

### Rockefeller / John Dee / Sentrum Scene

Repo basis:

- `rockefeller` is defined as an established concert venue in central Oslo with significance for Norwegian and international live music.
- `john_dee` is defined as an intimate stage attached to Rockefeller, known for new bands and alternative live music.
- `sentrum_scene` is defined as a larger concert and culture stage in central Oslo with broad music/scenekunst programming.

External basis: the official Rockefeller site brands itself as `Rockefeller - John Dee - Sentrum Scene`, identifies `Auditorium AS`, and lists the Oslo address `Badstugata 2, 0183 Oslo`.

Decision: safe to add separate collective anchors for the three venue roles:

- Rockefeller as live / concert ecosystem.
- John Dee as intimate stage / new-band ecosystem.
- Sentrum Scene as audience / large-stage ecosystem.
