## Research gate

- Johan Garmann took Rådhusgata 7 into use in 1647 and gave Garmanngården its name; Hans Nielsen Hauge was imprisoned in a rear building there from 1804 to 1807.
- Oscar II financed the re-erection of Gol stave church; Torolf Prytz prepared the initial general drawings, and Waldemar Hansteen led dismantling and reconstruction on Bygdøy.
- Waldemar Hansteen and Henrik Bull were the two architects of Centralbanken's 1915–1921 bank palace.
- Christian Brinch built Skippergata 3; Henrik Wergeland met Amalie Sofie Bekkevold in the family's tavern there in 1838.
- Gerhard Treschow commissioned Treschowgården in 1710 as a representative merchant property.
- Karen Toller built the present Oslo Ladegård in 1725; Caspar Herman von Storm added the lower side wing around 1770.
