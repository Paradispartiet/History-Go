## Physical configuration

The present installation has four consecutive chambers and a total lift of 26.6 metres. The staircase operates within Haldenkanalen between the water levels associated with Femsjøen and the Stenselva/Aspern route.

The power station uses the same hydraulic head but is a separate facility. The canonical coordinate therefore identifies the lock staircase rather than the power-house building or dam machinery.

## Historical interpretation

The first lock phase at Brekke belongs to the canal works of the 1850s. Sources vary in their wording about exact construction and completion dates, so the production does not force a single early completion year.

Flooding destroyed the early installation in 1861. Navigation was later restored, and the present system emerged from the 1917–1924 power development. The new water regime submerged the former Krappeto locks and concentrated the large lift at Brekke. The current four-chamber staircase was completed in 1924.

Timber transport remained a central canal function until 1982. The place is therefore modeled as infrastructure for navigation, timber, labour, water management and power—not only as a tourist attraction.

## Current use and access

Brekke remains an operational lock and active visitor destination. Current 2026 sources document scheduled tourist-boat passages, canoe activities and other seasonal use.
