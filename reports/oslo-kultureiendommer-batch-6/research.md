## Villa Romsli

No canonical History Go record was found for `Villa Romsli` or Odvar Solbergs vei 83.

Oslo kommune documents that the building was built by the Red Cross in the 1970s. In 1988 it was transferred to Romsås bydel for the symbolic price of one krone, with a condition that the property continue to serve public-benefit purposes. The building was first used for housing for people with intellectual disabilities and later as a volunteer centre with a youth club. Today Villa Romsli is an art centre with 12 studios.
