# Historie / Institusjonsledelse — prerequisite source-first

## Scope
Role: `historie/historie_institusjonsledelse` (`Avdelingsdirektør` / `Direktør`). This materializes the career foundation prerequisite, **not Role World completion**. The dedicated Role World remains responsible for final situated reputation.

## Canonical foundation
The role preserves the existing `role_model_v2` and work grammar rather than introducing a parallel role format. Its two bounded work loops are institutional governance (`samfunnsoppdrag -> risiko -> prioritering -> ressursallokering -> styring -> resultat -> korrigering`) and incident governance (`hendelse -> situasjonsbilde -> ansvar -> tiltak -> kommunikasjon -> etterkontroll`). The persistent work object is `samfunnsoppdrag_ressurs_og_risikologg`, which carries decisions, evidence, owners, waiting states, handoffs and bounded rework through existing Civication surfaces.

## Authority and appointment
The role may `styre institusjonen innen lov, delegasjon og samfunnsoppdrag`. It may not `overstyre lovverk`, `diktere faglige funn`, `skjule vesentlig risiko`, or `behandle institusjonens mandat som privat eierskap`. Entry is `appointment_required`. Both canonical salary rows retain `appointment_required` offer policy and `employer_appointment` entry route; the prerequisite does not create a shortcut around appointment, delegation or formal authority.

## People, places and working surfaces
Four role-scoped people are explicit fictional scenario actors, each with `canonical_person_ref: null`; they are not promoted into the factual People pool. Four concrete working surfaces cover governance, professional integrity, budget/prioritisation, and risk/emergency handoff. Their function is to make institutional leadership situated without inventing factual people or a new runtime state model.

## Mail and playable day
The canonical plan has 16 ordered steps and uses all nine standard mail types with empty fallback lists. The role has 15 role-scoped mails in total: four `job`, four `people`, and one each of `conflict`, `story`, `event`, `micro`, `followup`, `knowledge`, and `consequence`. Generic fallback is disabled. The same plan, work grammar and mail families carry the first playable day and repeatable work rhythm.

## Knowledge and History Go boundary
Functional knowledge includes the `historie` History Go badge and uses historical context, source criticism and provenance to improve questions and decisions. History Go does not grant appointment, delegation, budget authority, permission to override law, or authority to dictate professional findings. It is an evidence and learning affordance, not a governance substitute.

## Runtime and readiness boundary
This prerequisite uses existing Civication mail, Career, Scene Registry, Scenario People and readiness contracts; **no new runtime** is introduced. The intended prerequisite state is `playable`, runtime gate true, no missing Career components, `rollout_ready`, and exactly one remaining Role World-authored dimension: situated reputation. Cross-role materialisation is not required for rollout and no shared work object is invented.

## Fail-closed proof target
The prerequisite package is valid only when its focused contract, Career gameplay, rollout readiness, Scene Registry, Scenario People, full Civication suite, job learning-profile audit and job knowledge-requirements audit all pass together. Any failure must leave canonical materialised state unchanged and preserve only a diagnostic record. Role World completion is therefore a separate, later proof step rather than an implication of this prerequisite foundation.
