## Historical framing

Saugbrugsforeningen was founded in 1859 by sawmill owners associated with the Tista waterfalls. The company was created as the owners prepared for the removal of sawmill privileges and increasing competition.

The site and enterprise subsequently developed through several industrial phases, including:

- sawmilling and planing;
- mechanical pulp;
- cellulose;
- paper production;
- power and process infrastructure; and
- modern wood-processing and paper-industry operations.

The name Norske Skog Saugbrugs reflects the company's incorporation into Norske Skog in 1989. The retained `year: 1859` identifies the foundation of Saugbrugsforeningen, not the construction date of every building now inside the industrial area.
