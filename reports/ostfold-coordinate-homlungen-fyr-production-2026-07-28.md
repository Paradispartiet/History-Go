## Entity decision

The canonical marker represents Homlungen fyrstasjon as a protected lighthouse-station environment. Wikidata `Q3428819` and Kulturminne ID `86050` identify the station on the holm Homlungen. The legacy point was on Kirkøy and did not represent the lighthouse building or station complex.

The lighthouse house, outbuilding, former livestock and assistant functions, boathouse, landing and boat slip belong to the station environment. The coastal path from Skjærhalden, the rowboat crossing and present coastal-route accommodation are access and reuse layers rather than replacement coordinates.

## Historical model

`year: 1867` is the operational establishment year. The first light was shown on 1 October 1867. Lens upgrades in 1894 and 1915, automation and depopulation in 1952, holiday-home use in 1952–1997 and coastal-route-cabin use from 2008 are separate layers.

## Access and safety

The station lies on a small islet in Ytre Hvaler National Park. Public use depends on reservation, keys, weather and sea conditions. The route includes a roughly one-kilometre coastal path and a short rowboat crossing. Gameplay must never require unsafe rock, boat, landing, locked-building or technical-area access.
