## Wegnerpaviljongen i Frognerparken

Oslo kommune documents the protected pavilion on the hill called Utsikten in Frognerparken, near Frogner hovedgård. It was built at Blaafarveværket in 1824 and moved to Frognerparken by Benjamin Wegner in 1836 as a wedding gift to Henriette Wegner.

The pavilion is a small, physically discoverable object inside an already represented major park complex. A separate canonical map marker would overlap the existing `vigelandsparken` place and add little navigational value.

## Representation decision

Represent the pavilion as a Wonderkammer `actual_site_treasure` attached to `vigelandsparken`.

The chamber should emphasize:

- the 1824 origin at Blaafarveværket
- relocation to Frognerparken in 1836
- its role as a wedding gift from Benjamin Wegner to Henriette Wegner
- the classical octagonal temple form
- the painted ceiling inspired by the Pantheon in Rome
- recent conservation/restoration documented by Oslo kommune
