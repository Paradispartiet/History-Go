## Authored realism dimension

- Only `situated_reputation` is authored in this rollout.

## Government-specific authority

- Statssekretær remains bounded by actual assignment/delegation and cannot inherit ministerial or prime-ministerial authority.

- Statsminister leads and coordinates government but is not modeled as unlimited personal authority replacing ministers, collegial government, Parliament or other constitutional organs.
- History Go and the Politikk badge can improve historical and institutional questions but cannot supply current public_office_appointment, evidence, legal authority, budget authority, confidential access or a current decision.
