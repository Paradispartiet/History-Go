# Politikk / Organisasjonsarbeid — Role World rollout source-first

## Scope lock
This is a one-role controlled rollout for `politikk/politikk_organisasjonsarbeid`. Organisasjonssekretær remains a `direct` career offer with no invented appointment or qualification gate. Direct access to the job is not elected mandate, economic signature, unrestricted member-data access or authority to rewrite a decision.

## Authored delta
Only `situated_reputation` is newly authored. There is **no global reputation score**. Existing role model, four fictional People, four work surfaces, 16-step plan, persistent work object, work loops, waiting/handoff/rework and authority boundary are preserved. There is **No new runtime** and no parallel scene format.

## Audience model
Standing is separate for elected leadership/decision bodies, members/member service, local and county organizations/trustees, member-data/privacy/economy controls, volunteers/event staff/vendors, external partners/public contact, and private/internal organizational culture. Divergence is intentional: trust may rise in one audience while falling in another without becoming authority.

## Authority separation
History Go and Politikk-badge may improve questions about organizational democracy, local political history, representation, voluntary organization and institutional memory, but cannot provide a decision, elected mandate, member-data access, privacy basis, budget approval, contract authority or evidence of current authorization. Situated reputation cannot override the selected decision owner or turn secretariat proximity into democratic power.

## Cross-role
Cross-role need remains `not_required_for_rollout`; there is **no cross-role link** because no genuinely shared persistent work object is required for this rollout.

## Provenance and materialization
The season uses all **15 canonical** prerequisite mail references as provenance. The dramaturgical contract is **14 days × 4 phases = 56** unique beats, with every canonical ref used at least three times.

## Editorial uniqueness
This world is authored around member democracy, secretariat integrity, member-data/privacy, local-central handoff, volunteer capacity, event/resource realism, budget/contract authority and institutional memory. Structure reuses the proven Role World contract, while text, social actors, standing audiences, threads and delayed consequences are specific to Organisasjonsarbeid and do not copy the municipal-government world.
