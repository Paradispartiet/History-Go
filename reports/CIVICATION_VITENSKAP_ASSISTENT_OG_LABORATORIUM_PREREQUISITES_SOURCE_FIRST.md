# Vitenskap / Assistent og laboratorium — prerequisite source-first

## Scope lock
This package completes the existing canonical role scope `vitenskap_assistent_og_laboratorium` in place. It is prerequisite foundation work, not Role World completion, and creates no parallel career identity.

## Canonical career policy
- **Studentassistent**, **Vitenskapelig assistent**, **Forskningsassistent** and **Laboratorieassistent** remain `qualification_required`.
- Every title keeps `relevant_education_or_employer_qualification` and salary tier 1.
- Vitenskap-badge, XP, History Go, social standing, local familiarity or seniority cannot waive qualification, training, access, HMS/biosafety or authority boundaries.

## Persistent work
The role now owns the persistent laboratory work object `laboratoriearbeidslogg_prove_id_protokoll_instrument_hms_metadata_kvalitet_avvik_handoff_og_rework`. It keeps task, sample identity/provenance, qualification, protocol, instrument/HMS, raw data, metadata, quality control, deviations, handoff and bounded rework separately versioned. Waiting states are explicit and raw history is preserved.

## People and work surfaces
Four fictional scenario actors cover sample traceability, instrument/HMS/biosafety, data/metadata and quality/handoff. Four fictional work surfaces make the same boundaries operational; they are not canonical public Places or factual historical People.

## Mail and day one
There are **15 source mails** across all nine required types and a 16-step no-fallback plan. Day one starts only after the canonical qualification gate and then requires sample/provenance, protocol, local training/access where relevant and safety/instrument checks before measurement or handling.

## History Go boundary
History Go and the Vitenskap badge may improve historical context about laboratories, instruments, standardization, safety regimes and scientific practice. They do not prove today's sample identity, protocol, qualification, training, access, instrument status, HMS/biosafety, raw data, metadata or scientific conclusion.

## Situated reputation
The prerequisite package deliberately leaves the audience-specific situated-reputation realism layer unauthored. Standing may affect cooperation but never qualification, evidence, access, safety permission or authority. Dedicated Role World authoring remains required.

## Cross-role
`cross_role_need` may remain **candidate_when_shared_work_is_real** because laboratory work can genuinely share objects with research roles, but this prerequisite package invents no cross-role link. Any later link must prove an actually shared canonical work object.

## Runtime
**No new runtime.** Existing Civication scene, mail, career and History Go pipelines remain canonical.
