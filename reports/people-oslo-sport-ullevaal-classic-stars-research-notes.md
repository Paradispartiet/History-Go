# Ullevaal Stadion classic stars research notes

These five entries were selected because they are established Norwegian senior internationals from different eras and are suitable for direct `ullevaal_stadion` anchoring through Norway home internationals and national-team history.

- Einar «Jeja» Gundersen — early national-team scoring profile and one of Norwegian football's first major stars.
- Gunnar Thoresen — long-serving Norway forward and major post-war international profile.
- Roald «Kniksen» Jensen — celebrated Norwegian playmaker and international star of the 1960s.
- Harald «Dutte» Berg — central Norway midfielder and national-team profile across the 1960s and 1970s.
- Odd «Ivers» Iversen — prolific Norwegian goalscorer and long-serving senior international.

The entries use Ullevaal as the national-team and Norwegian football-history anchor. They do not claim that every defining club achievement occurred at Ullevaal.
