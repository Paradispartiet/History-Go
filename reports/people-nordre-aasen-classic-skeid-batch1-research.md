# Nordre Åsen classic Skeid batch 1 — research notes

## Scope

This batch adds five historical Skeid profiles as separate people files with `nordre_aasen_idrettspark` as the primary and only place anchor.

The repository place record defines Nordre Åsen as Skeid's home area, dates the place to 1952 and describes it as a historic club ground tied to club identity, local recruitment and talent development. The official place URL in the record is:

- https://www.skeid.no/om-stadion

Existing Nordre Åsen anchors were preserved:

- `skeid_fotball`
- `daniel_braaten`
- `morten_berre`

## Candidate gate

All five candidates had substantial Skeid careers after Nordre Åsen became the club's home area in 1952. The place link is therefore based on the documented Skeid home-ground period, not on a generic Oslo-football association.

### Harald Hennum

- Skeid player from 1953 to 1959.
- Norwegian Cup winner with Skeid in 1954, 1955, 1956 and 1958.
- 43 Norway caps and 25 international goals.
- Sources:
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harald_Hennum
  - Norwegian Football Federation profile referenced by the biography.

### Hans Nordahl

- Long-serving Skeid forward.
- Norwegian Cup winner with Skeid in 1947, 1954, 1955, 1956 and 1958.
- The four later titles fall inside the Nordre Åsen home-ground period.
- 19 Norway caps and eight goals.
- Source:
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Nordahl

### Finn Gundersen

- Began senior football with Skeid in 1952.
- Norwegian Cup winner in 1954, 1955 and 1956.
- Later played professionally for Hellas Verona and represented Norway in both football and ice hockey.
- Sources:
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finn_Gundersen
  - https://snl.no/Finn_Gundersen

### Kjell Kaspersen

- Many-year Skeid goalkeeper and captain.
- League champion with Skeid in 1966.
- 34 Norway caps and the only goalkeeper to score for Norway's senior men's national team.
- Sources:
  - https://www.vg.no/rampelys/i/qPK0gm/kjell-kaspersen-er-doed
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kjell_Kaspersen

### Jan «Jonas» Gulbrandsen

- 350 first-team appearances for Skeid.
- Norwegian Cup winner in 1954, 1955, 1956, 1958, 1963 and 1965.
- All six titles fall inside the Nordre Åsen home-ground period.
- Sources:
  - https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Gulbrandsen
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1965_Norwegian_Football_Cup
