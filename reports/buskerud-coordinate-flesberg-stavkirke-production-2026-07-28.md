## Identity decision

The canonical place represents the active Flesberg stave-church building. Direct building geometry is supported independently by Wikidata and Lokalhistoriewiki.

Separate components are:

- Flesberg churchyard;
- its standing stone-slab enclosure, tethering rings and stone crosses;
- Flesberg rectory approximately 200 metres north-west;
- the parish house erected on the church hill in 1977.

## Chronology decision

`year` changes from `1180` to approximate `1200`. The sources date the church to the final decades of the twelfth century or the beginning of the thirteenth century. An exact 1180 date is not documented.

The medieval stave church survives only in part. During the rebuilding of 1735, the high central space and eastern ambulatory wall were removed, while parts of the ambulatory walls, corner posts, sills, foundation timbers and portal remained. New log-built cross arms, chancel, sacristy and interior transformed it into the present cross church.

Further layers include the pulpit from the 1660s, the 1745 altarpiece, election-church use in 1814 and restoration in 1955–1965.
