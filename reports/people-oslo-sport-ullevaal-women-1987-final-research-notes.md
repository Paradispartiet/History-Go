# Ullevaal Stadion — women’s EURO 1987 final research notes

## Scope

Five players from Norway’s starting eleven in the 2–1 European Championship final victory over Sweden at Ullevaal Stadion on 14 June 1987.

## Primary source

Norges Fotballforbund match record:

- https://www.fotball.no/fotballdata/kamp/?fiksId=1698051

The match record confirms:

- Norway won 2–1.
- The match was played on 14 June 1987 with 8,408 spectators.
- Janne Andreassen started in goal.
- Heidi Støre started as captain.
- Trude Stendal, Gunn Nyborg and Kari Nielsen were in the starting eleven.
- Trude Stendal scored both Norwegian goals.
