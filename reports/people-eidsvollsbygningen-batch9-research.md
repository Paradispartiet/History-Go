# People of Places — Eidsvollsbygningen batch 9 research

## Scope

Batch 9 is the final selective pass for Eidsvollsbygningen.

The gate is deliberately stricter than simple membership in the Riksforsamlingen. Every accepted person must have:

1. explicit documented physical participation in the Riksforsamlingen at Eidsvoll in 1814, and
2. a clearly consequential role in constitutional debate, political institution-building or the representation of a historically important interest at Eidsvoll.

The batch is not filled to a fixed number. Candidates who are merely locally prominent, passive delegates or principally important only after 1814 are excluded.

## Explicit exclusions in the final pass

The following candidates were reviewed but not added because they do not clear the final significance threshold strongly enough:

- **Carl Adolph Dahl** — took positions in several debates, but Eidsvoll 1814 notes that he did not speak especially often and generally limited himself to short interventions.
- **Jørgen Aall** — historically significant later, but Eidsvoll 1814 explicitly describes him as more visible in later Storting activity than at the Riksforsamlingen.
- **Anders Rambech** — important later parliamentary career, but his direct activity at Eidsvoll was limited.
- **Andreas Aagaard Kiønig** — Eidsvoll 1814 describes him as keeping in the background throughout the assembly.
- **Hans Hein Nysom** — his most notable Eidsvoll contribution was limited to an intervention concerning the general law committee.
- **Georg Ulrich Wasmuth** — joined the independence side but did not actively participate in the debates.
- **Lars Larsen Forsæth** — did not participate in the debates.
- **Diderik von Cappelen** — remained in the background during the meetings.
- **Jacob Hersleb Darre** — did not participate especially actively outside the question of church property.
- **Hieronymus Heyerdahl** — almost never spoke during the meetings.
- **Peter Blankenborg Prydz** — did not actively participate in the negotiations.

## Final decision

Batch 9 adds four people and closes the Eidsvollsbygningen expansion under the current significance threshold.

The remaining unadded Eidsvoll representatives are intentionally not treated as missing data merely because they were present in 1814. A future addition should require new evidence of stronger place-specific historical significance, not simply completion of the full delegate list.
