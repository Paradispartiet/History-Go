## Scope

Five additional canonical people records for `holmenkollen_nasjonalanlegg`, selected only when there is a direct documented individual Holmenkollen ski jumping victory.

## Selected profiles

### Sven Hannawald (`sven_hannawald`)
- Won the individual Holmenkollen ski jumping competition in 2000.
- Primary source: Store norske leksikon, `Holmenkollrennene - vinnere`.
- SNL's dedicated Sven Hannawald article also states that he won Holmenkollrennet in 2000.

### Roar Ljøkelsøy (`roar_ljokelsoy`)

### Marius Lindvik (`marius_lindvik`)

## Place gate
