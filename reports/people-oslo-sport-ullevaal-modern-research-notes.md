# Ullevaal Stadion modern profiles — research notes

## Scope

Five modern Norwegian men’s national-team profiles are added as individual people files linked to `ullevaal_stadion`:

- Alexander Sørloth
- Joshua King
- Morten Gamst Pedersen
- Brede Hangeland
- Mohammed «Moa» Abdellaoue

## Relevance gate

Each person has a substantial Norway senior national-team career and a concrete Ullevaal anchor through goals, captaincy, qualification matches or long-term home-match involvement.

## Ullevaal anchors

- Alexander Sørloth: first senior international goal at Ullevaal in 2016 and multiple later home goals, including two against Estonia in 2025.
- Joshua King: scored against Cyprus at Ullevaal in the 2013 World Cup qualification campaign.
- Morten Gamst Pedersen: scored twice in the 4–0 World Cup qualifying win over Scotland at Ullevaal in 2009.
- Brede Hangeland: 91 senior caps and 48 matches as Norway captain, with Ullevaal as the principal home ground throughout his captaincy era.
- Mohammed Abdellaoue: two goals against the Czech Republic and the winner against Iceland at Ullevaal in 2011.

## Data model

- One person per JSON file.
- No batch people file.
- Empty `image` and `cardImage` fields remain explicit image-pipeline follow-up work.
