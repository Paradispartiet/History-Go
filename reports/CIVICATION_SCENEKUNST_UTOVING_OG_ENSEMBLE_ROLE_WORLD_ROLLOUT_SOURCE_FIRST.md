## Editorial uniqueness
This season is authored around the social mechanics of being a performer inside an ensemble: role and material ownership, note reception, partner consent, bodily load, stage cues, understudy/cover asymmetry, repeated performance, public exposure and fear that legitimate boundaries may affect future work. It does not copy the directing or production story; the shared shape is only the canonical Role World contract.

- **Eight** delayed consequences.

## Situated standing and authority

Public success cannot retroactively legitimize an unsafe, coercive or unsustainable process.
