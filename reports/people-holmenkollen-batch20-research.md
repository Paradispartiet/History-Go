# People of Places — Holmenkollen batch 20 research

## Place gate

This batch requires a directly documented individual competition victory in a Holmenkollen event. General skiing fame, Oslo association, medals elsewhere, or broad Holmenkollen association are not sufficient.

Primary source: Store norske leksikon, `Holmenkollrennene - vinnere`, updated 16 March 2026.

Fresh repository searches against post-batch-19 current `main` covered proposed ids, full names and relevant ASCII/diacritic variants. No existing canonical record was found for any of the five candidates.

All five pass the direct competition gate for `holmenkollen_nasjonalanlegg`. Each receives exactly one canonical person record with all documented Holmenkollen victories consolidated into that canonical record.
