# Holmenkollen People of Place batch 8 — research

## Scope

Five additional people for `holmenkollen_nasjonalanlegg`, selected only when the place gate is a direct documented Holmenkollen competition result.

## Selected profiles

### Torbjørn Falkanger (`torbjorn_falkanger`)
- Won the Holmenkollen ski-jumping competition in 1949 and 1950.
- Received the Holmenkollen Medal in 1952.
- Primary source used: Store norske leksikon, `Torbjørn Falkanger`.
- https://snl.no/Torbj%C3%B8rn_Falkanger

### Helmut Recknagel (`helmut_recknagel`)
- Won the Holmenkollen ski-jumping competition in 1957 and 1960.
- Received the Holmenkollen Medal in 1960 as the first recipient from outside the Nordic countries.
- Primary source used: Store norske leksikon, `Helmut Recknagel`.
- https://snl.no/Helmut_Recknagel

### Toralf Engan (`toralf_engan`)
- Won the Holmenkollen ski-jumping competition in 1962.
- Received the Holmenkollen Medal in 1962.
- Primary source used: Store norske leksikon, `Toralf Engan`.
- https://snl.no/Toralf_Engan

### Torbjørn Yggeseth (`torbjorn_yggeseth`)
- Won the Holmenkollen ski-jumping competition in 1963.
- Received the Holmenkollen Medal in 1963.
- Primary source used: Store norske leksikon, `Torbjørn Yggeseth`.
- https://snl.no/Torbj%C3%B8rn_Yggeseth

### Roger Ruud (`roger_ruud`)
- Won the Holmenkollen ski-jumping competition in 1981.
- The victory was one of his nine World Cup wins.
- Primary source used: Store norske leksikon, `Roger Ruud`.
- https://snl.no/Roger_Ruud
