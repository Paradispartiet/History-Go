## Why the park is canonical

Tomb's old manor main building no longer survives. Official Tomb history places it near today's internat and characteristic ponds, while Store norske leksikon describes the romantic park from around 1800 with ponds, canals, bridges and memorial features.

Rosehagen is a named, inspectable park geometry within this historical core. It represents the surviving manor landscape without pretending that the demolished house has a verified footprint.

## Identity resolution

The record represents:

- Tomb's former manor and estate landscape;
- the preserved park, ponds and yard structure;
- the transition to agricultural boarding school from 1939;
- the continuing school and farm environment;
- surviving historic components such as the old grain store.

It does not represent:

- a standing manor house;
- an exact archaeological footprint of the demolished main building;
- only the current school office or school point;
- Tomb church;
- the entire agricultural property as gameplay space.

## Year interpretation

The canonical year `1500` is retained only as an approximate roots layer for the demolished old main building. SNL states that the building probably had origins in the sixteenth century.

The year does not date:

- Rosehagen;
- the park as a whole;
- the current school buildings;
- the agricultural school;
- today's farm operation.

## Historical and current layers

- The estate has medieval roots.
- The old main building probably contained sixteenth-century fabric.
- The romantic park was developed around 1800.
- The old manor house was demolished late in the nineteenth century.
- The former grain store survives as Galleri Gamle Tomb.
- The property was acquired for school purposes in 1938.
- Agricultural boarding-school operations began in 1939.
- Tomb remains an active upper-secondary school and farm.

- Publicly promoted attractions do not make the complete property unrestricted.
- Tasks must avoid school-only and internat areas unless explicitly open.
- Tasks must not enter active fields, livestock areas or machinery zones.
