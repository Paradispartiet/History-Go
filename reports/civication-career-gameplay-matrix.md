## Interpretation

- `architecture_only` is not an error: it names work worlds that have been designed but cannot yet drive a workday.
- `partial` is intentionally broad and includes strong content packages whose full offer/day/consequence/salary path is not yet proven.
- Noncanonical namespaces are retained as support content but are excluded from career statuses and rollout planning.
- Only the generated status may be used in planning. A Badge tier, roleModel or FWG must not be called playable on its own.
