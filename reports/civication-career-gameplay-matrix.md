## Interpretation

- `architecture_only` is not an error: it names career-eligible work worlds that have been designed but cannot yet drive a workday.
- `partial` is intentionally broad and includes career-eligible content packages whose full offer/day/consequence/salary path is not yet proven.
- Canonical `noncareer` worlds retain gameplay content but have `career_status: not_applicable`; they are excluded from status counts, component debt and rollout planning.
- Noncanonical namespaces are retained as support content and are likewise excluded from career statuses and rollout planning.
- Only the generated career status may be used in planning. A Badge tier, roleModel, mailPlan or FWG must not be called playable on its own.
