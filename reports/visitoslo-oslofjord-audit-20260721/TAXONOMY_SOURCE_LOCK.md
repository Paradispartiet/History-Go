## Content locks

### Heggholmen

Treat the place as the named Heggholmen island identity, not as a synonym for the entire Gressholmen–Heggholmen–Rambergøya system. Core layers are Heggholmen lighthouse, the former paint/lacquer and soap-industry environment, surviving worker/functionary buildings, wharf and industrial traces, and the physical connection to the neighbouring islands.

Recommended emne direction:
- `em_his_spor_materialitet`
- `em_his_historiske_lag_i_byrom`
- `em_his_kulturminner_bevaring`

### Rambergøya

Treat protected coastal nature as primary. The former shooting range shared with Gressholmen and later soil/contamination management are human-use history layers that explain why present nature management is not a pristine-nature story.

Recommended emne direction:
- `em_natur_kyst_okosystemer`
- `em_natur_arter_habitat_mangfold`
- `em_his_historiske_lag_i_byrom`

### Ormøya

Treat Ormøya as an inhabited island urban form. Core layers are the transition from holiday destination to housing, bridge access, Swiss-style villas, Ormøy church as a neighbourhood landmark and the wartime observation/air-warning structure. Do not fold Malmøya into this record.

Recommended emne direction:
- `em_by_boligstruktur`
- `em_by_historiske_lag_i_hverdagsrom`
- `em_by_byidealer_og_plantradisjoner`

### Malmøya

Treat protected coastal geology and nature as primary. Lime/cement production, early summer houses and later year-round settlement are secondary historical-use layers. Solvikbukta is a sub-place/content layer and must not replace the island identity.

Recommended emne direction:
- `em_natur_kyst_okosystemer`
- `em_natur_arter_habitat_mangfold`
- `em_his_historiske_lag_i_byrom`

### Nakholmen

Treat the cabin colony and seasonal settlement pattern as primary. The place should explain how a fjord island became a highly organised part of Oslo's seasonal urban life. Nature reserves are important constraints and landscape layers, but the primary canonical reading is the cabin-island settlement form.

Recommended emne direction:
- `em_by_boligstruktur`
- `em_by_historiske_lag_i_hverdagsrom`
- `em_by_byidealer_og_plantradisjoner`

### Steilene

Treat the source-level identity as the Steilene archipelago, not a single arbitrary islet. Core content is the lighthouse station established in 1837, later automation/de-staffing and present cultural-heritage/kystled use. Additional petroleum or industrial claims require their own explicit source support before becoming core quiz facts.

Recommended emne direction:
- `em_his_spor_materialitet`
- `em_his_historiske_lag_i_byrom`
- `em_his_kulturminner_bevaring`

### Langøyene

Treat present-day island nature and recreation as primary, but make the landfill-to-remediation transformation indispensable. The current island form itself is historically produced: Nordre and Søndre Langøy were connected by waste filling, and the area later required major environmental remediation before reopening.

Recommended emne direction:
- `em_natur_kyst_okosystemer`
- `em_his_historiske_lag_i_byrom`
- `em_his_spor_materialitet`

### Lindøya

Treat the cabin colony and seasonal settlement system as primary. Core content is the growth of summer cabins from the 1920s, the distinctive colour/architectural planning tradition and the relationship between dense seasonal settlement and protected island nature.

Recommended emne direction:
- `em_by_boligstruktur`
- `em_by_historiske_lag_i_hverdagsrom`
- `em_by_byidealer_og_plantradisjoner`

### Ingierstrand bad

Treat the whole public bathing complex as the place identity. Core content is the 1934 functionalist bathing environment by Ole Lind Schistad and Eyvind Moestue, Oslo's ownership/public-recreation role, later restoration and protection. The restaurant building is one component and must not redefine the canonical place scope.

Recommended emne direction:
- `em_his_spor_materialitet`
- `em_his_historiske_lag_i_byrom`
- `em_his_kulturminner_bevaring`

### Bleikøya

Treat protected island nature as primary while preserving the strong social-history layers: the former children's coastal sanatorium, later cabin settlement and old farm environment. Do not reduce the island to Bleikøykalven or to the cabin colony alone.

Recommended emne direction:
- `em_natur_kyst_okosystemer`
- `em_natur_arter_habitat_mangfold`
- `em_his_historiske_lag_i_byrom`

### Ulvøya

Treat Ulvøya as a year-round residential island shaped by bridge access and planned villa subdivision. Sydstranda and protected nature are important public/natural layers but do not replace the island's primary residential urban-form identity.

Recommended emne direction:
- `em_by_boligstruktur`
- `em_by_historiske_lag_i_hverdagsrom`
- `em_by_byidealer_og_plantradisjoner`

## Source hierarchy

Production should prefer current official or institutional sources for factual claims, then strong local-history/reference sources. Canonical taxonomy files guide category/emne selection but are not the primary source for visible historical claims.

Primary source families used in this pass:

- Oslo kommune: current island, recreation, environmental-remediation and municipal-property documentation.
- Kartverket SSR: exact named-place identities for Heggholmen, Rambergøya and Ingierstrand bad.
- Kystverket: Steilene lighthouse history and present cultural-heritage use.
- Oslo byleksikon: strong local-history reference for Ormøya, Malmøya, Nakholmen, Lindøya, Bleikøya and Ulvøya.
- OpenStreetMap: exact physical geometry for eight island/island-group objects, used for coordinates rather than as the sole historical-content source.
