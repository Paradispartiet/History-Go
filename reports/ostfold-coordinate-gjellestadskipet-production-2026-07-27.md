## Coordinate decision

The canonical marker now follows the named archaeological-site point for the Gjellestad ship grave and settlement remains on Viksletta. NIKU's georadar work identified the ship form in 2018, and the 2020–2021 excavation confirmed the physical ship-burial context. Research and public interpretation place the ship within a broader landscape of monumental graves, longhouses and long-term elite activity.

The legacy point was approximately 2.64 kilometres east and had no Coordinate Source Contract.

## Identity and representation

The combined record covers both the buried Gjellestad ship and the visible monumental mound Jellhaugen. The ship-grave and settlement-remains field is the primary coordinate anchor because it is the central modern archaeological discovery. Jellhaugen remains a separate secondary anchor approximately 175.6 metres south and is included in the retained gameplay radius.

The wider georadar and central-place landscape extends beyond the 360-metre circle. The radius is not an excavation boundary, georadar survey polygon, archaeological locality, legal protection area or property boundary.
