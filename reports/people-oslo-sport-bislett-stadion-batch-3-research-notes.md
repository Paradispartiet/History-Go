# People Oslo sport Bislett Stadion batch 3 research notes

## Batch 3 anchors

- Steve Cram: anchored to Bislett Stadion for the 1500 m world record in Oslo on 16 July 1985.
- Saïd Aouita: anchored to Bislett Stadion for the 5000 m world record in Oslo on 27 July 1985.
- Meseret Defar: anchored to Bislett Stadion for the 5000 m world record at Bislett Games on 15 June 2007.
- Tirunesh Dibaba: anchored to Bislett Stadion for the 5000 m world record at Bislett Games on 6 June 2008.
- Eric Heiden: anchored to Bislett Stadion for the 1000 m speed-skating world record at the 1978 world sprint championships.

## Data-shape decisions

- `placeId`: `bislett_stadion` for all five entries.
- `places`: one-item array containing `bislett_stadion` for all five entries.
- `image` and `cardImage`: intentionally empty strings; no external images were added.
- `category`: `sport` for all entries.
