## Persistent work and rhythm

The persistent editorial object is one named `faglig_prioriterings_og_kvalitetslogg`: a versioned record of incoming work, evidence/method constraints, capacity, priority, delegation, risk, decision status, handoff owner, waiting state and rework. It is a persistent editorial object carried through existing mail/runtime surfaces; **no new runtime** state or parallel scene format is introduced.

The role-specific rhythm is intake → capacity assessment → priority/delegation → waiting for evidence/mandate/competence → handoff → quality control → delivery → rework and learning. Waiting is explicit, and a handoff names the owner, the unresolved risk and the next checkpoint.

## Knowledge and authority

History Go knowledge may improve source criticism, historiographical awareness, context discrimination and the ability to identify when a claim needs stronger evidence. It cannot grant appointment, line-management authority, budget authority, specialist competence or permission to dictate a historical conclusion.

The leader may allocate work and prioritize inside delegated limits, but may not overrule law/delegation, hide capacity risk, pressure a particular historical conclusion or pretend that management authority substitutes for subject expertise.
